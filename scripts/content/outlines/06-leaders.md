# Course Outline — AI for Business Leaders & Executives

## Course Meta
- **Folder:** `06-leaders` (content dir: `scripts/content/courses/06-leaders/`)
- **Tier:** ₹3,000
- **Duration:** ~40 hours · 24 missions
- **Earning Math:** Prevent ₹5,00,000+ bad SaaS purchases; reduce departmental costs 15-20%
- **Top 5 Unique Assets:**
  - Build vs. Buy decision matrix spreadsheet
  - AI Acceptable Use Policy template (Indian companies)
  - Vendor vetting questionnaire (20+ questions)
  - AI ROI Dashboard template
  - Scripts for addressing employee fear of replacement
- **India Context Hooks:**

  - Cost-conscious Indian board expectations
  - Upskilling legacy workforces
