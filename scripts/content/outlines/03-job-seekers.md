# Course Outline — AI for Job Seekers & Career Transitioners

## Course Meta
- **Folder:** `03-job-seekers` (content dir: `scripts/content/courses/03-job-seekers/`)
- **Tier:** ₹2,500
- **Duration:** ~45 hours · 24 missions
- **Earning Math:** Securing a job 2-3 months faster saves ₹50,000+; freelance gigs add ₹10,000–₹20,000/month
- **Top 5 Unique Assets:**
  - ATS Resume Scorer with line-by-line rewrites
  - Naukri/LinkedIn DM template generator
  - Voice behavioral interview simulator
  - 30-day Skill Gap syllabus generator
  - Salary negotiation role-play bot
- **India Context Hooks:**
  - Naukri.com/Instahyre realities
  - Gap-year explanation scripts
  - CTC vs. in-hand salary

## Notes for Content Generation

- Each mission expands every sub-module in its outline (typically 5) using `### Sub-module K · Title`.
- Every submodule ends with a `**Example Quiz**` block (6 questions).
- Tone: simple English, Indian examples, warm Hinglish, ₹ earning angle.
- See `scripts/content/prompts/{slug}-prompt.md` for the full generation prompt.
