# Course Outline — AI for Aspiring Entrepreneurs

## Course Meta
- **Folder:** `04-entrepreneurs` (content dir: `scripts/content/courses/04-entrepreneurs/`)
- **Tier:** ₹3,000
- **Duration:** ~50 hours · 24 missions
- **Earning Math:** Save ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 in agency costs; accelerate first revenue by 2-3 months
- **Top 5 Unique Assets:**
  - Indian Angel Investor Database (prompt-assisted)
  - WhatsApp waitlist builder template
  - AI pitch-deck narrative checker
  - Indian legal contract drafter with disclaimers
  - Unit economics calculator (INR)
- **India Context Hooks:**
  - Family pressure to get a real job
  - WhatsApp for early customers
  - GST/LLP basics

## Notes for Content Generation
- Each mission file = `module-NN.md` (NN = mission number, zero-padded).
- Each mission expands every sub-module in its outline (typically 5) using `### Sub-module K · Title`.
- Every submodule ends with a `**Example Quiz**` block (6 questions).
- Tone: simple English, Indian examples, warm Hinglish, ₹ earning angle.
- See `scripts/content/prompts/{slug}-prompt.md` for the full generation prompt.
