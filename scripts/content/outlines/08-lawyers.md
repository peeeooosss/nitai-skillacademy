# Course Outline — AI for Lawyers & Legal Professionals

## Course Meta
- **Folder:** `08-lawyers` (content dir: `scripts/content/courses/08-lawyers/`)
- **Tier:** ₹3,000
- **Duration:** ~45 hours · 24 missions
- **Earning Math:** Save 10+ hours/week → 2-3 extra clients/month (₹30,000–₹50,000/month billable)
- **Top 5 Unique Assets:**
  - BCI Compliance & AI guideline module
  - Legalese-to-Plain-English client communication pack
  - Contract risk-flagging template (Indian Contract Act)
  - Secure non-training AI tool recommendations
  - Chronological timeline builder from client notes
- **India Context Hooks:**
  - BCI advertising restrictions
  - Summarizing BNS/IPC provisions
  - Tier-2/3 city client expectations
