# Course Outline — AI for Farmers & Agri-Entrepreneurs

## Course Meta
- **Folder:** `12-farmers` (content dir: `scripts/content/courses/12-farmers/`)
- **Tier:** ₹1,500
- **Duration:** ~35 hours · 24 missions
- **Earning Math:** Save 10-15% on inputs; increase realization 5-10% (₹10,000–₹30,000/acre/year)
- **Top 5 Unique Assets:**
  - Voice-first vernacular prompt guides
  - Fake Yojana scam detection checklist
  - eNAM and WhatsApp agri-bot integration guides
  - Natural farming (Jeevamrutha) recipe generator
  - Direct-to-consumer WhatsApp sales templates
- **India Context Hooks:**
  - eNAM Mandi price volatility
  - PM-KISAN and state subsidy navigation
  - Delayed monsoon contingency

## Notes for Content Generation
- Each mission file = `module-NN.md` (NN = mission number, zero-padded).
- Each mission expands every sub-module in its outline (typically 5) using `### Sub-module K · Title`.
- Every submodule ends with a `**Example Quiz**` block (6 questions).
- Tone: simple English, Indian examples, warm Hinglish, ₹ earning angle.
- See `scripts/content/prompts/{slug}-prompt.md` for the full generation prompt.
