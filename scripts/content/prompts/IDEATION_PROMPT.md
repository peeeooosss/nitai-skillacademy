```
You are a senior skills-marketplace strategist + curriculum architect for NITAI AI Skill Academy, an Indian AI-learning platform (https://nitai-skillacademy.netlify.app). I need a rigorous ideation session BEFORE writing final course content.

## BACKGROUND

Platform already has:
- 12 AI courses (one per audience). Each course = "missions"; each mission file holds 4-5 submodules ("### Module K · Title") each with learning objectives, theory, examples, a practice challenge, and a 6-question mini-quiz. Quizzes auto-check pass (70%), assignments are graded, learners earn XP/credits.
- "AI Didi" in-course AI mentor (context-aware per mission), live-session hosting, admin approval-based enrollment, certificates.
- Pricing target: ₹1,500–₹3,000 per course, lifetime access, India-first audience (mostly mobile data users on WhatsApp).

## CURRENT STATE (thin today — must become 24 missions × 6 phases per course)

01-school-students (8 missions): What is AI/learning buddy · smart prompting · homework & projects · AI art · science & math · presentations/events · ethics (real vs fake) · showcase project
02-college-students (10): foundations/roadmap · academic prompting · research/lit review · assignments & citations · data analysis · coding assistants · agents basics · mini-project · internship portfolio · placement prep
03-job-seekers (6): career mapping · AI-resume & LinkedIn · skill gaps/30-day plan · mock interviews · job-search automation · brand/portfolio/negotiation
04-entrepreneurs (8): idea hunt · market validation · business model · MVP/landing page · pitch deck · lean ops (marketing/finance/legal) · no-code automation · launch & first customers
05-professionals (6): role workflows · automating repetitive work · pro prompting · reports/emails/PPTs · department workflows · measuring gains
06-leaders (4): AI landscape · adoption roadmap · vendors/build-vs-buy · change leadership
07-journalists (5): newsroom toolkit · research & fact-checking · story structure · video/audio/visuals · ethics & transparency
08-lawyers (6): legal research · drafting/summarising · contract review · fact patterns · confidentiality & governance · AI-enabled practice
09-doctors (6): clinical orientation · documentation · literature review · patient communication · diagnostics support & safety · data privacy
10-creators (6): content engine setup · scripting/ideation · shooting/editing · calendars & repurposing · growth analytics · monetisation
11-homemakers (6): AI journey welcome · first conversations · everyday life (cooking/kids/home/money) · micro-business · freelance gigs · community teacher roadmap
12-farmers (6): welcome · weather/soil/crop advisory · market prices & schemes · WhatsApp + voice AI how-tos · agri-business value-add · farm-year planning

## TARGET FORMAT (fixed; don't fight it)

- Every course becomes 24 missions in 6 phases (4 missions per phase).
- Each mission = 4-5 submodules (Concept → Tool walkthrough → India-context use case → Hands-on project → Money/safety angle).
- Tone: simple English, Indian examples, occasional Hinglish warmth; mobile-app workflows (WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube).
- The money story matters in every course: ₹/month earning potential, not vague "opportunity".

## WHAT I WANT FROM YOU — in this exact output structure

### SECTION A — Platform-wide differentiators (rank top 15)
A ranked table: Idea · What learner gets · Why it justifies ₹3,000 · Build effort (Low/Med/High). Only ideas we can realistically ship (content, PDFs, prompt packs, live events, communities, challenges, certificates, referrals, EMI billing, etc.). Flag the 5 must-dos.

### SECTION B — Per course (repeat this block 12 times)
For each course:
1. One-line "who exactly this course saves" (the buyer's pain)
2. Table of 6 phases: Phase name · Learner promise after phase · Missions 1-4 titles
3. Mission titles in order (24 numbered, marketable, not generic)
4. TOP 5 unique things ONLY this course will do (per-audience special assets — e.g. mock-listener graders, board-exam prompt safaris, mandi price trackers, bar-compliance guardrails — be specific, not generic)
5. 3 India-context hooks (sensoria: festivals, exams, crops, placements, price culture)
6. Earning math: realistic ₹/month outcomes for this learner and the module count creating them
7. Gap analysis: current missions — KEEP (and retitle) / UPGRADE / RETIRE
8. Bonus assets worth printing/downloading for this audience

### SECTION C — Pricing & positioning
- Assign each of the 12 courses a tier (₹1,500 / ₹2,000 / ₹2,500 / ₹3,000) with one-sentence reasoning
- How to bundle (e.g., family pack, women's pack, campus pack)
- 5 launch ideas (webinars, WhatsApp demos, referral credits, early-bird, EMI)

Rules: be decisive (pick, don't offer alternatives), every mission title must sound worth paying ₹100+, total answer is LONG — go the distance, don't summarise.
```
