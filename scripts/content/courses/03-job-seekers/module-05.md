# Mission 5: Job-Search Automation & Applications

## Learning Objectives
- Build an organised job-search system you can run daily
- Automate sourcing, tracking, and follow-ups with AI
- Write tailored applications from role to application letter
- Match your resume to each posting in minutes
- Run a productive 90-day application cadence

---

## Job Search Is a System, Not a Feeling

Scattered searching burns you out. This mission turns your search into a **daily system**: sourced → tracked → tailored → followed-up → reviewed. AI accelerates every step; your discipline makes it work.

## Step 1: Your Job Search Command Center

Set up in your tools:

- **A tracker** (Sheet/Notion/Airtable) with columns: Company | Role | Link | Source | Status (sourced/applied/response/interview/offer/rejected) | Applied date | Follow-up date | Notes.
- **Saved searches** in job boards + alerts (daily digest, not spam).
- **A daily routine:** fixed 45 minutes: 15 source → 15 tailor → 15 apply + log. Tomorrow's hour beats today's panic.

Automating this cycle is a portfolio story in itself: build the tracker yourself in Mission 6 of Course 02 if you didn't already.

## Step 2: Automate Sourcing (Smarter, Not Harder)

- **Search prompts:** "Find 5 [role] openings for a [profile] in [city/remote] on [board], each with: link, team, must-haves, salary if shown." Verify links before applying.
- **Quality filter:** "Of these, which 3 best match my resume highlights [paste]? Rank by fit and flag any red flags in the post (e.g., outdated stacks)."
- **Company signals:** "Check these companies' hiring pages/roles for: remote policy, growth stage, tech/stack, and culture hints." (Fast, manual check; don't trust AI's claims about current openings — click through.)

**Filter first, apply selectively.** Applying to junk is a tax on your focus.

## Step 3: The Apply-Matching Pipeline

For each serious posting, run a 3-step tailor:

1. **Role-match brief:** "From this posting [paste] make a 5-line brief: must-haves, nice-to-haves, 2 terms the recruiter likely screens for, and what my resume should lead with."
2. **Resume pass:** feed your master resume + the brief → output a tailored one-page version (verify all changes against truth).
3. **Cover letter (short, real):** 150 words: hook from the posting + 2 proof points + why now.
   "Draft a 150-word cover letter for [role] at [company] using my resume + the posting. Lead with a specific connection to their product/team; end with a confident ask. No clichés."

## Step 4: Follow-Ups That Don't Annoy

Follow-up converts silent applications into replies. System: **if no response in 7–10 days, send one short, specific follow-up.**

Template + personalisation:
> "Subject: [Role] application — [Name] / [date applied]. One-line reminder of the role, one line of specific interest (their product/post), one-line polite ask for timeline. 80 words."

Ask AI to draft 3 tones (warm, direct, friendly) — then choose and personalise. Do NOT mass-mail identical follow-ups to 50 companies; targeting wins.

## Step 5: The 90-Day Cadence & Review

Weekly (30 min): review tracker, count your funnel (sourced → applied → replies → interviews). Adjust:
- 0 replies from 10 tailored apps? Fix resume/LinkedIn (Mission 2) or widen geography/role tier.
- 0 responses after follow-ups? Improve targeting, not volume.
- Interviews but no offers? The weak spot is Mission 4 — more reps.

Log one-liners weekly: *"Applied 12, 3 replies, 2 interviews, 1 second-round. Lessons: X."* The system keeps you calm and honest.

## Application Honesty Rules

- Apply only where you genuinely qualify (or meet ~70% of must-haves) — chasing 1% fits wastes your best hours.
- Never fake skills, dates, or roles; gap periods are fine and explainable.
- Don't let AI ghost-write applications empty of your specifics — generic letters are the first thing recruiters skip.

## Key Takeaways

- A 45-min daily system (source→tailor→apply→log) beats episodic panic.
- Filter with AI, verify links, apply selectively — quality over volume.
- Tailor each application: brief → resume pass → 150-word letter.
- Follow up at day 7–10 with one short, specific note.
- Review the funnel weekly and adjust the weak stage.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Build your tracker** (or import a template) with the columns above. Add your saved searches + alerts.
2. **Do a data day:** use the sourcing prompts to find 10 real postings, filter to your top 4, and log them all in the tracker.
3. **Tailor-run:** for 2 of the 4, run the apply-matching pipeline and produce a tailored resume + 150-word cover letter.
4. **Write 2 follow-ups** (different tones) for already-applied roles, ready to send at day 10.
5. **Start the cadence:** do today's 45-minute routine now and enter the first row in your tracker.
