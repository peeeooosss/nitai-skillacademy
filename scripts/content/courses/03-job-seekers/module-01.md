# Mission 1: Career Mapping — AI-Augmented Roles That Fit You

## Learning Objectives
- See the career landscape as "role + AI" instead of "AI replaces roles"
- Identify AI-augmented roles that match your skills and experience
- Map your transferable skills into a target role shortlist
- Assess the AI skills required for your top roles
- Build a 90-day career-adaptation plan

---

## The "Role + AI" Mindset

Headlines scream "AI will take jobs." The reality is more useful than the fear: **AI replaces tasks, not (whole) jobs — so jobs become "human skill + AI tooling."** Almost every career — from for example HR to banking to marketing to operations — is becoming *augmented*.

So the question shifts from *"Will AI replace me?"* to *"Which version of my job uses AI most?"* The people who answer that win the transition.

## Four Kinds of AI-Augmented Career Paths

1. **Deep-Hands Roles** — you build AI systems (data, ML, prompt/agent engineering).
2. **Tool-Powered Domain Roles** — you use AI daily inside your current field (marketing, legal, finance, HR, ops...) — the largest pool of jobs.
3. **Process & Workflow Roles** — you automate work for teams (automation specialists, AI operations).
4. **Human-Advantage Roles** — trust, judgement, relationships + AI underneath (management, sales, coaching, care). Human judgment becomes MORE valuable, not less.

**Where are you today?** Your current experience is transferable: if you've done logistics, you can become a logistics-automation specialist. If you've written drafts, you can become an AI-content lead. Your past work is a runway, not a cage.

## Steps to Map Yourself to a Role

### Step 1: Inventory your skills
List your 8 strongest skills — split into **hard** (tools, processes, languages, technical skills) and **soft** (communication, problem solving, reliability, stakeholder handling). Be honest; recruiters will check.

### Step 2: Generate role shortlist with AI
> "Act as a career strategist. My background: [skills, industry, experience]. Recommend 8 realistic AI-augmented roles for me — 2 deep-hands, 3 tool-powered in my domain, 2 process/automation, 1 human-advantage. For each: typical tasks, gross salary range, and 2 core AI skills needed."

Rank those 8: *feasible for me with ≤90 days effort* + *motivating*.

### Step 3: Pick a Tier-1 target
Choose **one** primary role you'll pursue (your "North Star") and one backup. Write it as a sentence: *"I'm transitioning from [now] → [target role] by building [2 AI skills] and [1 proof project]."*

### Step 4: Gap analysis
For your Tier-1:
> "List the 10 most common skills in [My target role] job postings. Mark which I have and which are gaps, and recommend the 2 cheapest gaps to close first."

### Step 5: The 90-day plan
Structure: **days 1–30** learn/close gaps → **days 31–60** build 1 proof project (Mission 5 uses this) → **days 61–90** apply with tailored materials (Missions 2–4) and interviews (Mission 6).

## The Job-Post Extraction Trick

Read real posts, not guesses. Paste a target role's posting into AI:

> "Extract from this job description: the 5 must-have skills, the 3 'nice-to-have' skills, the salary band if present, and what my resume should emphasize to match. Then list any requirements the post contradicts (unrealistic or outdated)."

Recruiters write signals in postings; learning to decode postings is a job-search skill in itself.

## Keep Your Story Honest

- Don't claim AI expertise you don't have yet — you'll be asked to *show* it within weeks.
- Your transferable-skills story must be *demonstrable*: "I ran women's safety logistics; now automate onboarding flows" — with proof in Missions 4–5.
- Job search is numbers game + story game. This plan gives you both.

## Key Takeaways

- AI replaces tasks, not jobs → every role becomes "human + AI."
- Four paths: deep-hands, tool-powered domain, process, human-advantage.
- Map → shortlist (8) → rank → one Tier-1 target → gap analysis.
- Use a 90-day plan: learn, prove, apply.
- Decode real job postings; keep your story honest and demonstrable.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Skill inventory:** write your 8 skills (4 hard, 4 soft) with one proof example each.
2. **Get your 8 roles:** use the career-strategist prompt. Rank them and choose Tier-1 + backup with a one-line "why."
3. **Gap analysis:** paste 2 real job postings for your Tier-1 into the extraction prompt. List your top 3 gaps.
4. **Write your plan:** sketch your 90-day plan (learn / prove / apply) with 3 concrete actions for each 30-day block.
