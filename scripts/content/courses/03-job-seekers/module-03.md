# Mission 3: Skills Gap Analysis & Your 30-Day Learning Plan

## Learning Objectives
- Turn gaps into a prioritised, learnable skills map
- Build a focused 30-day upskilling plan with AI
- Find quality free learning resources (not just lists)
- Track progress and build quick proof as you learn
- Turn learning into demonstrable portfolio items fast

---

## From "A Need" to "A Skills Map"

You know your gaps from Mission 1. Boxes in your head don't get hired — skills maps do. By end of this mission you'll have: a **prioritised gap list**, a **30-day plan**, and **proof milestones** you can show.

## Step 1: Prioritise Gaps (3 buckets)

Not all gaps are equal. Sort them:

- **Must-close (A):** appear in most target postings; cheap to learn. Do A first.
- **Nice-to-have (B):** appear sometimes; nice for differentiation. Optional weeks 3–4.
- **Reality-checks (C):** nice words that need years (e.g., "deep ML research") — *reframe*, don't chase. You don't need to become an ML researcher to get hired in AI-augmented roles.

> **The reframing rule:** if a gap would take >1 year to close, pivot the target role slightly instead.

## Step 2: Generate a Learning Map

> "Act as a learning designer. Given my gaps [list A] and target role [role], produce a 30-day plan: each week 1 focus area, 3 concrete learning tasks per day, 1 practical mini-project per week, and free resources (official docs, courses, communities). Prioritise hands-on practice over passive videos."

Rules for your plan:
- **Week 1:** foundations of the main skill (e.g., AI basics or your domain's core tool).
- **Week 2:** one hands-on mini-project → becomes a portfolio item.
- **Week 3:** second skill + second mini-proof.
- **Week 4:** integration — combine skills into ONE polished showcase project (this feeds Mission 5).

## Step 3: Find Quality Resources (Don't Trust the First List)

AI blithely lists "great courses." Filter them:

- **Official sources first** — the tool's own docs/tutorials beat third-party listicles.
- **Community proof** — a subreddit/discord/forum where practitioners actually answer questions.
- **Hands-on over passive** — anything with projects/labs > video marathons.
- **Free or cheap** (official free tiers, platform free courses, library access).
- **Verify before committing:** ask AI "what does [course] actually teach? hours? prerequisites? labs? last updated?"

The trap: collecting 20 courses and finishing zero. Pick **one track per skill**, finish it, ship the proof.

## Step 4: Track Progress To Keep It Honest

- **Log daily:** 2 lines max in a tracker ("learned X, practised Y, stuck on Z").
- **Weekly proof checkpoint:** every week, something visible must exist: a config, a sample, a small project, a notebook.
- **Recap with AI weekly:** paste your weekly log; AI drafts a progress summary + suggests next 3 actions. Reading your own progress (compounded) is powerful motivation.

## Turn Learning Into Evidence Fast

The moment you learn something, weaponise it:

- Finished a tool module? → Build one 20-minute use-case with YOUR real data.
- Learned a prompt pattern? → Save the template you actually use (from Mission 2 of Course 02 if you did it).
- Solved a problem? → 5-line "how I did it" post for your LinkedIn (validates learning + builds presence).

Every week should end with **something visible**. Passive consumption isn't proof; shipped bits are.

## The 30-Day Commitment Contract

Print or pin this and check daily:

> **I will spend 45 min/day for 30 days on [skill].**
> **Week 1:** foundations. **Week 2:** mini-project #1. **Week 3:** skill #2 workout. **Week 4:** integration showcase.
> **Weekly proof:** something visible every Friday.
> Non-negotiable: 1 rest day/week; ask for help before burning 2 hours stuck.

## Key Takeaways

- Prioritise gaps: A (must-close), B (nice), C (reframe — don't chase years of depth).
- One 30-day plan: foundations → mini-project → skill #2 → integrate.
- Verify resources before committing; one track finished beats 20 started.
- Track daily, ship weekly proof, recap with AI.
- Every week produces something visible for your portfolio.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Prioritise:** split your Mission 1 gaps into A/B/C buckets. Reframe any C gap (pivot role slightly if needed).
2. **Build the plan:** generate your 30-day plan with AI, then edit it so each week has 1 visible deliverable.
3. **Verify 2 resources:** confirm 2 courses/tracks actually teach what AI claimed (check hours, prerequisites, hands-on, recency).
4. **Start today:** do Day 1 of your plan NOW and log it in your tracker (2 lines). Pin your commitment contract where you'll see it daily.
