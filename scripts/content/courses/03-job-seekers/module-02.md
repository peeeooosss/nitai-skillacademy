# Mission 2: AI-Resume & LinkedIn Rebuild

## Learning Objectives
- Rewrite your resume to match a target role with AI
- Build achievements with numbers and the PAR method
- Use AI to tailor one resume version per application
- Rebuild your LinkedIn profile for recruiter searchability
- Pass the 6-second and ATS readability tests

---

## Why Your Resume Fails Before a Human Reads It

Recruiters and **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** skim in seconds. Your resume fails silently if it isn't: **scannable** (structure no machine fights), **relevant** (keywords a human expects), and **quantified** (achievements, not duties). AI speeds all three — but you must steer it with truth.

## Use the PAR Method First

Every bullet should follow **PAR** (aka STAR without the filler):

- **P — Problem** 📍 the challenge
- **A — Action** ⚙️ what you did (with AI, when true)
- **R — Result** 📈 measured outcome

**Weak (duty):** "Handled customer complaints."
**Strong (PAR):** "Resolved 40+ complex escalations/quarter, cutting repeat calls 25% by introducing a 5-step triage checklist."

Prompt for transformation:
> "Rewrite each of my raw duties as PAR bullets. Keep them honest, specific, and under 18 words. Quantity where I gave real numbers; leave a [ ] placeholder where I must verify or fill a metric."

## The AI Resume Workshop (3 Passes)

**Pass 1 — Match to target role.** Paste your resume + your Mission 1 gap list:
> "Rewrite my resume to target [role]. Infer the 10 skills that role wants; emphasize them using my real experience only. Flag every place you beefed a claim so I can verify."

**Pass 2 — Quantify & keywords.** Add ATS keywords from the job post; improve action verbs; kill weak language ("responsible for", "helped with").

**Pass 3 — Tailor per application.** For each serious application, feed the job description and adjust. One size never fits.

## The Honesty Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

- **Never let AI invent numbers, titles, companies, dates, or skills.** Every claim must survive "show me."
- If AI suggests stronger language, first check it's still **true**.
- Do NOT "keyword-stuff" skills you can't answer in an interview — that's a trap, not a hack.
- Keep one honest "AI use" choice: you can note "I use AI-assisted workflows" in skills *if it's true* — it's increasingly valued, not penalised.

## Resume Structure That Compiles

- **Header:** name, phone, email, city, LinkedIn, portfolio/GitHub (no photos in most markets).
- **Summary (3 lines):** your story sentence (Mission 1) + 3 value points.
- **Skills:** 6–10 grouped (tech / tools / soft).
- **Experience:** reverse-chronological, PAR bullets, 4–6 per role.
- **Education + Certifications + Projects.**
- One page for junior levels; two max otherwise.

## LinkedIn: Build for Recruiters

LinkedIn is searchable, so it needs *keywords in the right places*:

- **Headline (120 chars):** "Operations Specialist → Process Automation | AI Workflows | [Your niche]" — role + transition + tools.
- **About:** 3 short paragraphs: hook, proof (numbers), transition story. Ask AI for 3 drafts, then edit to sound like you.
- **Experience:** mirror your resume PAR bullets.
- **Featured section:** your proof project (Mission 5) front and centre.
- **Skills:** add the 10 from your target posting; get endorsements from 3 real people.
- **Opening:** settings → turn on "Open to Work" for recruiters (recruiters-only visibility to stay private at work).

Prompt help:
> "Rewrite my headline and About for [role]. 120-char headline; About in 3 scannable paragraphs. Use my real facts only. Flag suggested keywords separately."

## The Two Readability Tests

1. **The 6-second test:** glance at your top-of-page for 6 seconds — if your value isn't obvious, fix. Have one friend (who knows nothing of your work) do it.
2. **The ATS test:** pasting your resume text into a plain-text editor should reveal clean, logical structure — no tables/photos/columns that confuse parsers. Or use a resume checker tool.

## Key Takeaways

- PAR bullets beat duty bullets: Problem → Action → Result.
- Run 3 passes: match role, quantify keywords, tailor per application.
- Never let AI invent metrics or titles — verify everything.
- Build LinkedIn for search: keyword-rich headline, About with proof, Featured proof project.
- Test with 6-second scanning + ATS plain-text check.

## Practice Challenge

1. **PAR rewrite:** take your 3 weakest current bullets and rewrite them as PAR with AI — then verify every number is real (or replace with a place the AI flagged).
2. **Target-tailored resume:** produce one resume version matching your Tier-1 role, keywords included.
3. **LinkedIn pass:** rewrite headline + About with AI, edit for your voice, and add your top 10 target-role skills.
4. **Tests:** run the 6-second test with one person and the ATS plain-text check on your resume.
