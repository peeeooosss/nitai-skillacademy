# Mission 4: AI Mock Interviews & Case Studies

## Learning Objectives
- Prepare a tight 90-second "tell me about yourself"
- Run realistic mock interviews with AI, then a human
- Use AI to debrief each practice and fix weak spots systematically
- Crack common technical + behavioural question patterns
- Prepare for take-home case / assignment-style rounds

---

## The Interview Is a Skill — Practise It Like One

Nobody is "naturally good" at interviews; great interviewees are just well-practised. With AI you can practise at 8am, at midnight, repeatedly — and the reps are what matter.

## Your 90-Second Opening (Master This First)

Every interview opens with "tell me about yourself." Perfect your structure:

1. **Now** — current situation + what you're known for (1 line).
2. **Transition** — why you're moving toward X using your background (1 line).
3. **Proof** — one achievement or project with a number (1–2 lines).
4. **Here** — why this role/company and what you bring (1 line).

Prompt to build it:
> "Draft a 90-second 'tell me about yourself' from my resume [paste], structure: now / transition / proof / here. Keep it natural, specific, under 160 words. Then give me 2 alternative openings."

Write it in YOUR words, time it out loud, and have one person hear it.

## The AI Mock-Interview Loop (Run 3 Session Types)

Create a repeating loop — practise, debrief, fix:

**Session 1 — Behavioural (STAR):**
> "Act as a senior interviewer at [company type] hiring for [role]. Ask me 5 behavioural questions one at a time, e.g., conflict, failure, deadline, feedback, disagreement. After each answer score 1–5 on clarity/structure/specificity, then ask one follow-up."

**Session 2 — Technical / skill-specific:**
> "Ask me 4 technical questions about [role skill/tool stack]. Include 1 scenario and 1 'you're asked to build X' question. After each, point out where an expert would push further."

**Session 3 — Case / take-home style:**
> "Give me a realistic 40-minute case: [open-ended business/data problem for my role]. Wait for my approach, then push back on weak spots like a hiring manager."

**Always answer out loud** into the mic. Written answers don't train your mouth.

## The Post-Session Debrief (Where the Real Gain Is)

After EVERY session get your 3-fix list:
> "Based on this entire interview, give me exactly 3 weaknesses to fix and 1 strength to scale. For each weakness, one concrete drill to improve it this week."

Keep a **growing fix-list file** across sessions. Track the same weakness shrinking — that's your practice working.

## Pattern Library: The 5 Question Archetypes

Nearly all interview questions reduce to five patterns. Prepare answers for each:

1. **Weakness/mistake** → STAR with a real failure + what you changed. Never "I work too hard."
2. **Behavioural "tell me about a time..."** → STAR, 60–90 seconds, result with numbers.
3. **Why us / why this role** → research the company (below), 2 specific reasons.
4. **Problem-solving scenario** → talk through your approach step-by-step out loud (hiring managers hire the thinking).
5. **Your questions for us** → always have 2–3 smart questions (use AI to draft, then pick).

**Company research with AI:**
> "Summarise [company]: what they do, their products, recent news (verify!), culture signals, and 3 sharp questions I should ask the interviewer that show real interest."

## Preparing for Take-Home Tasks

Many roles send an assignment (analyse data, build a small automation, prepare a document). Beat it professionally:

- **Restate scope in 2 lines** before starting; ask 1 clarifying question if allowed — recruiters love the precision.
- **Deliver over-explained:** assumptions, method, results, next steps — not just the answer.
- **Record your process** (even 5 bullet points). Interviewers ask how you approached it — that's the real test.
- Never use AI to fake the deliverable; use it to structure and polish your own work, and **say so if asked**.

## Key Takeaways

- Perfect your 90-second opening: now / transition / proof / here.
- Run 3 mock types (behavioural, technical, case) and answer OUT LOUD.
- Debrief every session into 3 fixes; read your fix-list each new session.
- Master the 5 question archetypes + company research.
- Take-homes: restate scope, over-explain your method, be honest about AI use.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Build your 90-second intro** with AI, rewrite in your voice, time it out loud 5 times.
2. **Run 3 AI sessions** (behavioural, technical, case) over this week. Answer out loud, record your 3-fix list after each.
3. **Human mock:** do 1 real practice interview with a friend/senior/recruiter — the AI reps make this one count. Track the same 3 fixes with them.
4. **Company prep:** pick your top target company, run the research prompt, and write your 3 "questions for them."
5. **Add to package:** save your 90-sec intro, fix-list file, and top-5 archetype answers into your placement folder from Course 02.
