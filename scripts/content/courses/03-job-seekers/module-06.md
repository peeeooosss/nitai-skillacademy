# Mission 6: Personal Brand, Portfolio & Offer Negotiation

## Learning Objectives
- Build a small, sharp personal brand that proves your skills
- Turn your learning into public proof (without oversharing)
- Handle the offer stage: evaluate, negotiate, decide with clarity
- Negotiate salary and terms with AI-prepared scripts
- End the course with a working application-to-offer system

---

## Your Brand IS Your Proof

Nobody needs you to be famous. You need to be **findable and provable**: when someone searches your name, they should see evidence that you can do the thing you claim. That endpoint is a one-page personal site + a tidy GitHub + a focused LinkedIn.

## Build the Three-Proof Brand (Fast)

**Proof 1 — Your project page.** One-page site (Netlify/Vercel/GitHub Pages — free): your story sentence, 2–3 STAR projects from Course 02 Mission 9, the 60-sec demo, and a contact CTA. 2–3 hours max; don't rebuild the internet.

**Proof 2 — GitHub discipline.** Clean repos: README, screenshot, honest AI-use line, 2+ commits per project. Add the automation you built in Course 02 Mission 7 if relevant. Messy history + empty README signals the opposite of pro.

**Proof 3 — Content that costs 15 minutes.** One newsletter/commit-frequency note, or 1 short post/week: "made X with [tool], this is what I learned; here's the mini-repo." Regular tiny posts (build-in-public) beat one perfect essay — consistency compounds.

**AI help:** "Draft a one-page site copy using my portfolio notes [paste]: hero line, 3 project blurbs, footer. Keep it 200 words of real content." Then you edit, host, screenshot.

## Handling the Interview→Offer Bridge

When interviews progress, protect yourself:

- **Reference-check the company too** — salary bands on the web/apps, Glassdoor-style signals, current employees via LinkedIn.
- **When asked your expected salary:** give a researched band, not a number into the void:
  > "For my profile and this market, I'm targeting [X]–[Y]. What's the band you're working with?" — turn it into a two-way conversation.

## Negotiation: Prepare a Script, Keep It Human

Most negotiators lose by being unprepared, not by being rude. Build your script with AI, keep it warm:

> "Act as a salary negotiation coach. My offer: [details]. Market band for [role/location]: [X–Y]. I have [strengths/proof]. Give me a 3-step negotiation script: 1) positive acceptance, 2) a researched counter within the band, 3) a graceful ask on [one non-salary item, e.g., learning budget, remote days, title]. Keep it under 150 words and polite."

**Rules that actually work:**
- **Never accept on the spot.** "Thank you — I'd like to review the total package and respond by [day]." Always.
- **Negotiate the total package,** not just salary: bonus, learning budget, remote days, equipment, title, notice/start date.
- **One counter, then listen.** You don't need to win every dollar; you need a respectful exchange.
- **Know your walk-away number** *before* the call. Uncomfortable but saves regret.

## Accept, Decline, or Walk Away

A decision framework when offers (or drafts) arrive:

1. **Package review:** salary + benefits + growth + commute/remote + team quality + learning curve. Score 1–5 each, weight by your goals.
2. **Compare to your Tier-1 story** (Mission 1): does this role accelerate the path you chose?
3. **Sleep on it.** Impulse "yes" and fear-based "no" are both unreliable states.

If you decline, do it graciously and keep the connection: a polite note now is a network asset later.

## One Believable Application-to-Offer System

You now have every piece. Assemble the loop:

- Brand (this mission) → Job system (Mission 5) → Mock reps (Mission 4) → Materials (Mission 2) → Proof projects (Course 02). Each feeds the next.
- Weekly rhythm: 4–5 quality applications + 1 brand update + 2 mock reps + 1 funnel review.
- **Never break the loop during rejections.** Rejections are data; the system is the plan.

## Key Takeaways

- Brand = findable + provable: project page, tidy GitHub, small consistent content.
- Research the company and keep an offer-time negotiating script ready.
- Never accept on the spot; negotiate the total package, know your walk-away number.
- Decide with the package-score + Tier-1 cultural fit check, and sleep on it.
- Your application-to-offer loop only works if you run it weekly, even through rejections.

## Practice Challenge (Final)

1. **Brand build:** publish your one-page proof site with your 2–3 projects + demo link + honest AI-use line. Share the URL.
2. **Brag file:** start a saved notes doc "wins.txt": every positive reply, testimonial, mock score, and lesson. You'll need its confidence later.
3. **Negotiation drill:** write your script for the salary band you want, then run a mock negotiation with AI (it plays the hiring manager) and one human.
4. **Offer framework:** pre-fill your package-score sheet with the criteria that matter to YOU (weighted).
5. **Commit to the loop:** set your weekly counter (e.g., "5 apps / 1 brand update / 2 mocks") and run Month 1. You're hired-ready — the reps start now. 🚀
