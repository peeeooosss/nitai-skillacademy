# Mission 4: Starting a Micro-Business with AI

---
### Module 1 · A Business You Can Start from Home

Many homemakers already have the skills to earn: cooking, tailoring, teaching, crafts, or organising. A micro-business starts small, from home, with what you have.

AI helps you plan, price, and promote - so you spend less time worrying and more time earning.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand what a micro-business is
- See the skills you already have
- Start small from home
- Use AI to plan your business

## Micro-Business Basics

- Small, home-based, low cost to start
- Built on a skill you already have
- Grows step by step
### Why
- You do not need a big loan
- You can start today
- Small steps reduce risk

## What AI Helps With

- Naming and describing the business
- Pricing your products
- Writing messages for customers
- Planning and tracking
### The rule
- Start with a real skill
- Keep costs low
- Use AI for planning and words

## The Business Prompt

```text
I want to start a small home business. My skills are [list].
Suggest 3 micro-business ideas I can start with little money
and how AI can help with each.
```
- Three business ideas
- Low-cost starts
- How AI helps each

## Key Takeaways

- A micro-business starts small and from home
- You can build it on a skill you already have
- You do not need a big loan to begin
- AI helps with naming, pricing, and promoting
- Small steps reduce risk and build confidence

## Practice Challenge

1. List five skills you already have
2. Ask AI for three business ideas
3. Pick the one you like most

**Example Quiz**

1. What is a micro-business?
- A) A small home business
- B) A big company
- C) A shop in the market
- D) A factory

**Answer:** A — A micro-business is small, home-based, and low cost to start.

2. What should it be built on?
- A) A skill you already have
- B) A big loan
- C) A factory
- D) Luck

**Answer:** A — Build your business on a skill you already have.

3. What does AI help with?
- A) Naming, pricing, and promoting
- B) Cooking the product
- C) Paying taxes
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — AI helps you name, price, and promote your business.

4. Why start small?
- A) It reduces risk
- B) It looks bad
- C) It earns less
- D) It is slow

**Answer:** A — Starting small reduces risk and lets you grow step by step.

### Module 2 · Step 1: Find Your Skill and Your Customer

A business works when your skill meets a real need. Find what you do well and who needs it.

AI helps you match your skills to customers and understand what they want.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify your strongest skill
- Find who needs it
- Understand customer needs
- Match skill to demand

## Skill and Customer

- What you do well and enjoy
- Who has a problem you can solve
- What they are willing to pay for
### Why
- Skill plus demand equals business
- Knowing the customer guides everything
- Real needs mean real sales

## Finding Your Match

- List your skills and who needs them
- Ask AI who buys such services
- Talk to a few possible customers
### The rule
- Start with a real need
- Know your customer
- Keep it simple

## The Skill-Match Prompt

```text
My skills are [list]. Help me find the best customer for each
skill, what problem it solves for them, and how to test if
they would pay for it.
```
- Skill and customer matches
- The problem each solves
- A way to test demand

## Key Takeaways

- A business works when your skill meets a real need
- Identify what you do well and enjoy
- Find who has a problem you can solve
- Know what customers are willing to pay for
- Test demand before you invest

## Practice Challenge

1. List your top three skills
2. Write who needs each one
3. Ask two people if they would pay for it

**Example Quiz**

1. What makes a business work?
- A) Skill meeting a real need
- B) A big loan
- C) A shop
- D) Luck

**Answer:** A — A business works when your skill solves a real customer need.

2. What should you find first?
- A) Who needs your skill
- B) A shop
- C) A loan
- D) An office

**Answer:** A — Find the customers who have a problem your skill solves.

3. What should you know?
- A) What customers will pay for
- B) The weather
- C) Their OTP
- D) Their passwords

**Answer:** A — Know what customers are willing to pay for your service.

4. What should you do before investing?
- A) Test demand
- B) Spend big
- C) Quit
- D) Borrow

**Answer:** A — Test whether people will pay before investing your money.

### Module 3 · Step 2: Name and Describe Your Business

A good name and a clear description help customers remember you and understand what you offer.

AI can suggest names and write a simple description you can use on WhatsApp, posters, or social media.

## Learning Objectives

- Choose a good business name
- Write a clear description
- Make a simple tagline
- Be easy to remember

## Naming Your Business

- Short and easy to say
- Related to what you offer
- Easy to remember and spell
### Why
- A clear name builds trust
- Easy names spread by word of mouth
- A tagline says what you do

## Describing It

- Say what you offer and for whom
- Keep it to one or two lines
- Add what makes you special
### The rule
- Simple and clear
- Say the benefit
- Keep it short

## The Naming Prompt

```text
I am starting a home business selling [product or service]
to [customers]. Suggest 10 short business names, a one-line
tagline, and a simple description I can use on WhatsApp.
```
- Ten name ideas
- A tagline
- A short description

## Key Takeaways

- A good name is short, clear, and easy to remember
- A tagline says what you do in one line
- Describe what you offer and for whom
- Keep the description to one or two lines
- AI can suggest names, taglines, and descriptions

## Practice Challenge

1. Ask AI for ten business names
2. Pick your favourite
3. Write a one-line description

**Example Quiz**

1. What makes a good name?
- A) Short and easy to remember
- B) Long and complex
- C) Hard to spell
- D) Random

**Answer:** A — A short, clear name is easy to remember and share.

2. What does a tagline do?
- A) Says what you do in one line
- B) Lists prices
- C) Shows your OTP
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — A tagline tells customers what you do in one short line.

3. What should a description include?
- A) What you offer and for whom
- B) Your password
- C) Your bank details
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — Describe what you offer and who it is for, briefly.

4. How long should the description be?
- A) One or two lines
- B) Ten pages
- C) One word
- D) A book

**Answer:** A — Keep your business description to one or two clear lines.

### Module 4 · Step 3: Make Your Products or Menu

Whether you sell food, crafts, or services, you need a clear menu or product list with prices that earn you a profit.

AI helps you plan what to make, work out costs, and set fair prices.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan your products or menu
- Work out your costs
- Set prices that earn a profit
- Keep quality consistent

## Planning Your Offer

- A short list of what you sell
- Prices for each item
- What makes each special
### Why
- A clear list helps customers choose
- Prices must cover your costs
- Quality keeps customers coming back

## Pricing for Profit

- Add up your ingredient and material costs
- Add your time and a small profit
- Round to easy prices
### The rule
- Price above cost
- Keep quality steady
- Start with a small menu

## The Menu Prompt

```text
I sell [product]. My cost per item is about [amount] and my
time is [hours]. Help me set a fair price that earns a
profit, and suggest a short menu of 5 items to start.
```
- A fair selling price
- A short starting menu
- Profit explained simply

## Key Takeaways

- You need a clear menu or product list with prices
- Add up costs, time, and a small profit when pricing
- Price above your costs to earn a profit
- Keep quality consistent to keep customers
- Start with a short menu and expand later

## Practice Challenge

1. List five products or dishes
2. Work out the cost of each
3. Set a price that earns a profit

**Example Quiz**

1. What do you need to sell?
- A) A clear menu with prices
- B) A shop
- C) A loan
- D) A licence

**Answer:** A — A clear menu or product list with prices helps customers choose.

2. What must a price cover?
- A) Costs and a profit
- B) Only costs
- C) Nothing
- D) Only profit

**Answer:** A — Your price must cover costs and include a small profit.

3. How many items should you start with?
- A) A short list
- B) A hundred
- C) None
- D) One thousand

**Answer:** A — Start with a short menu and expand as you learn.

4. What keeps customers coming back?
- A) Consistent quality
- B) High prices
- C) Rude service
- D) Waiting

**Answer:** A — Consistent quality keeps customers returning and recommending you.

### Module 5 · Step 4: Reach Customers on WhatsApp and Social Media

You do not need a big shop or expensive ads. WhatsApp status, local groups, and simple posts can reach many customers.

AI helps you write clear, warm messages and posts that get replies.

## Learning Objectives

- Reach customers with simple tools
- Write effective messages
- Use WhatsApp and social media
- Grow by word of mouth

## Reaching Customers

- WhatsApp status and groups
- Simple posts with clear photos
- Friends and neighbours telling others
- A short, warm message
### Why
- Free tools reach many people
- Clear messages get replies
- Word of mouth is powerful

## Writing Messages

- Say what you offer and the price
- Keep it short and friendly
- Add one clear photo
### The rule
- Short and clear
- Warm and polite
- Always include how to order

## The Marketing Prompt

```text
Write a short, friendly WhatsApp message and a social media
post for my home business selling [product] at [price].
Include how to order and keep it warm and simple.
```
- A WhatsApp message
- A social media post
- Clear ordering steps

## Key Takeaways

- WhatsApp and social media can reach many customers for free
- Keep messages short, clear, and warm
- Always say what you offer, the price, and how to order
- One clear photo helps a lot
- Word of mouth from happy customers grows your business

## Practice Challenge

1. Write a WhatsApp status for your product

3. Send it to ten people

2. How should messages be?

- D) Confusing

**Answer:** A — Keep messages short, clear, and warm to get replies.

3. What must a message include?
- A) How to order
- B) Your OTP
- C) Your password
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — Always tell customers what you offer, the price, and how to order.

4. What grows your business?
- A) Word of mouth

**Answer:** A — Happy customers telling others grows your business steadily.

### Module 6 · Step 5: Track Orders, Money, and Growth

A business grows when you keep track of orders, money in and out, and what sells best.

AI helps you make simple records and see where to improve.

## Learning Objectives

- Track orders and payments
- Keep simple money records
- See what sells best
- Plan your growth

## Keeping Records

- Orders taken and delivered
- Money in and money out
- Which items sell best
### Why
- Records show if you are earning
- They show what to make more of
- They help you plan ahead

## Simple Tracking

- Keep a daily note of sales
- Add up income and costs weekly
- Note your best sellers
### The rule
- Record every sale
- Check profit, not just sales
- Grow what works

## The Tracking Prompt

```text
Help me set up a simple record system for my home business:
orders, money in, money out, and best sellers. Show me a
simple weekly summary I can keep in a notebook.
```
- A simple record format
- A weekly summary
- Best-seller tracking

## Key Takeaways

- Track orders, money in, money out, and best sellers
- Records show whether you are actually earning
- Check profit, not just total sales
- Grow the products that sell best
- A simple notebook or app is enough

## Practice Challenge

1. Start a daily sales note
2. Add up income and costs for a week
3. Note your best-selling item

**Example Quiz**

1. What should you track?
- A) Orders, money, and best sellers
- B) Only sales
- C) Only costs
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — Track orders, money in and out, and which items sell best.

2. Why keep records?
- A) To see if you are earning
- B) To waste paper
- C) To show off
- D) To confuse

**Answer:** A — Records show whether your business is actually earning a profit.

3. What should you check?
- A) Profit, not just sales
- B) Only sales
- C) Only stock
- D) Only weather

**Answer:** A — Check your profit, not just how many items you sold.

4. What should you grow?
- A) What sells best
- B) What does not sell
- C) Nothing
- D) Everything equally

**Answer:** A — Grow the products that sell best and drop the rest.
