# Mission 4: Starting a Micro-Business with AI

Your AI skills can now help you earn. This mission teaches you how to find a small business idea that fits your home, validate it with AI, and set up simple sales — starting from your kitchen table.

## Learning Objectives

- Discover a micro-business idea that matches your skills, time, and home.
- Use AI to test whether the idea has real demand in your area.
- Get AI to build your starter kit: menu, pricing, and an opening WhatsApp message.
- Know the very first steps to sell one product in the next seven days.

## The Core Idea

The best micro-business is the one that fits your life: it uses the skill you already have, fits the hours you can actually give, and needs little money to start. AI's role is to help you think that idea through properly — what to sell, what to charge, how to describe it, how to announce it.

### Where Ideas Come From

Homemakers already hold some of the most valuable skills in the market:

- **Cooking skills** → tiffin service, festival sweets, pickles, baked goods, catering small orders.
- **Craft skills** → tailoring, embroidery, handmade jewellery, diya decoration, mehendi.
- **Knowledge skills** → teaching, coaching, tuition for children or for homemakers learning new skills.
- **Management skills** → helping small shops with WhatsApp ordering, accounts, or social posts.

Ask yourself: *"What do people already compliment me on? What do I do faster than my neighbours? Who already pays for things like this?"*

### Validate With AI Before You Invest

Before buying raw material for a big batch, talk to AI:

- "People in my area order homemade sweets for festivals and birthdays. What are the most popular items and what price range works in small-town India?"
- "What questions should I ask potential customers before starting a tiffin service?"
- "What are the risks, costs, and rules of selling homemade food from home in India?"

AI gives you a planner's perspective — more ideas and risks on the table than you'd think of alone. It does **not** know your local market; confirm big decisions by talking to real neighbours.

### Build Your Starter Kit With AI

Once you have an idea, let AI build the essentials:

1. **Menu/service list** — "List 5 dishes for my tiffin service with prices for a 5-day weekly plan, giving a market-rate price range."
2. **Pricing sanity** — "A tray of 24 kaju katli costs me 350 rupees in ingredients and 3 hours of time. What should I charge per tray and per piece?"
3. **WhatsApp opening message** — "Write a friendly WhatsApp message announcing my pickle service to neighbours. Include prices and how to order. Keep it short and warm."
4. **Simple order form** — "Make a table for my orders with name, dish, quantity, date, amount, deposit, and delivery."

### The Seven-Day Loop

- **Day 1–2:** pick idea, validate with AI + 5 real conversations.
- **Day 3:** build menu/pricing with AI, get ingredients for a small trial batch.
- **Day 4:** make the trial, photograph it, ask 3 friends to taste and give honest feedback.
- **Day 5:** send your WhatsApp announcement to neighbours and family groups.
- **Day 6–7:** take your first small orders; deliver; ask buyers for a photo/review you can reuse.

## Real-World Examples

**Example 1 – The festival sweet box.** Anita loved making kaju katli for family. With AI she priced a 24-piece box against market rates, wrote a WhatsApp message, and pre-sold 10 boxes for the festival within a week of announcement — with just her pressure cooker and a 500-rupee raw material run.

**Example 2 – The tiffin idea.** Maya, a strong cook, asked AI whether a 5-weekday tiffin service made sense in her area. AI prompted her with the real questions to ask: who is the customer (single professionals, students), what they pay, dietary preferences. After 6 conversations she started small — 5 customers, Mondays to Fridays.

**Example 3 – The mehendhi artist.** Poonam realised her mehendi designs were her most praised skill on WhatsApp groups. AI helped her write a portfolio caption, a price list, and a booking message. Orders for wedding season started without a single rupee spent on advertising.

## Key Takeaways

- Your home skills are marketable skills; start from what people already praise in you.
- Validate with AI *and* with real conversations — AI broadens your thinking, neighbours confirm your market.
- AI builds your starter kit: menus, pricing checks, WhatsApp announcements, order tables.
- Start tiny: one product, a trial batch, five customers. Growth comes later.
- Never spend real money on a big batch before testing demand.

## Practice Challenge

**Task:** Produce a one-page business plan for your micro-idea.

1. Write your idea in one sentence: *"I will sell [product/service] to [type of customer] in my area."*
2. Ask AI: "What are the most important questions I should research before staring this?" — write the top 5.
3. Ask AI to draft your **WhatsApp announcement message** with prices and how to order.
4. Have 5 real conversations with neighbours/family about your idea and note their feedback.
5. Calculate a trial-batch budget with AI: ingredients, packaging, and your time.
