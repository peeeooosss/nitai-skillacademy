# Mission 1: Welcome to Your AI Journey — What AI Can Do for You

---
### Module 1 · What AI Really Is (No Scary Movies)

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence. Forget the scary movies - think of it as a helpful assistant that lives inside your phone and understands plain language.

It can talk, write, explain, plan, and find information in seconds. It is like a knowledgeable friend who never gets tired and never makes you feel small for asking.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand what AI is in simple words
- Let go of fear about AI
- Know what AI can and cannot do
- Feel confident to try it

## AI in Simple Words

- A helper inside your phone or computer
- It understands normal language
- It answers in seconds, any time
### Why
- You do not need to be technical
- You talk to it like a person
- It is a tool, not a robot

## What AI Cannot Do

- It cannot feel or truly understand you
- It can be wrong, so you must check
- It cannot replace your own judgment
### The rule
- AI helps, you decide
- Always check important answers
- Your experience still matters most

## The First Prompt

```text
Explain in very simple words, like to a friend, what AI can
do for a homemaker. Give 5 everyday examples I can try on
my phone today.
```
- Simple explanation
- Five everyday examples
- Things you can try today

## Key Takeaways

- AI is a helpful assistant, not a scary robot
- You talk to it in normal language
- It answers in seconds and never tires
- It can be wrong, so always check
- AI helps; you stay in charge

## Practice Challenge

1. Open an AI app on your phone
2. Ask it one simple question
3. Notice how it replies like a person

**Example Quiz**

1. What does AI mean?
- A) Artificial Intelligence
- B) Automatic Internet
- C) A phone brand
- D) A type of TV

**Answer:** A — AI stands for Artificial Intelligence, a helpful assistant.

2. How do you talk to AI?
- A) In normal language
- B) In code
- C) In special words
- D) You cannot

**Answer:** A — AI understands plain, everyday language.

3. What should you do with AI answers?
- A) Check them
- B) Trust them fully
- C) Ignore them
- D) Delete them

**Answer:** A — AI can be wrong, so always check important answers.

4. Who stays in charge?
- A) You
- B) The AI
- C) The phone
- D) The internet

**Answer:** A — AI helps, but you always make the final decision.

### Module 5 · Staying Safe and Private Online

AI is helpful, but you must protect your private information. Some things should never be shared with any app.

This lesson teaches simple safety rules that keep you and your family safe.

## Learning Objectives

- Know what never to share
- Protect family privacy
- Spot scams and false promises
- Use AI safely

## What Never to Share

- Bank details, OTPs, and passwords
- Aadhaar, PAN, and ID numbers
- Full names and addresses of family
- Photos you would not want public
### Why
- Scammers use this information
- Privacy protects your family
- Safety comes before convenience

## Simple Safety Rules

- Never share OTPs with anyone, even if they sound official
- Do not put private documents into apps
- If an offer sounds too good, it usually is
### The rule
- No OTPs, ever
- No private documents in apps
- Trust your instinct

## The Safety Prompt

```text
Teach me the safety rules for using AI apps on my phone:
what I must never share, how to spot scams, and how to
protect my family. Keep it very simple.
```
- What never to share
- How to spot scams
- Family protection tips

## Key Takeaways

- Never share bank details, OTPs, or passwords
- Keep ID numbers and family details private
- No OTP is ever needed to receive money or help
- If an offer sounds too good, it usually is
- Safety always comes before convenience

## Practice Challenge

1. Write your own never-share list
2. Show it to a family member
3. Delete any app that asked for OTPs

**Example Quiz**

1. What should you never share?
- A) OTPs and passwords
- B) Recipes
- C) Homework questions
- D) Weather questions

**Answer:** A — Never share OTPs, passwords, or bank details with anyone.

2. What do scammers use?
- A) Your private information
- B) Your recipes
- C) Your hobbies
- D) Your questions

**Answer:** A — Scammers misuse personal information, so keep it private.

3. What if an offer sounds too good?
- A) It usually is a scam
- B) Take it fast
- C) Share your OTP
- D) Pay first

**Answer:** A — Offers that sound too good to be true are usually scams.

4. What comes before convenience?
- A) Safety
- B) Speed
- C) Money
- D) Fun

**Answer:** A — Always put safety and privacy before convenience.

### Module 6 · Your First Week with AI

The best way to learn is to use AI a little every day. In one week you can build a comfortable habit.

This lesson gives you a simple seven-day plan to get started.

## Learning Objectives

- Follow a simple first-week plan
- Build a daily AI habit
- Try one new task each day
- Feel confident by week end

## Your Seven Days

- Day 1: ask five simple questions
- Day 2: use AI for cooking
- Day 3: use AI for your child
- Day 4: make a shopping list
- Day 5: try a budget
- Day 6: try a small task you dislike
- Day 7: review what helped most
### Why
- Daily practice builds confidence
- Small tasks feel easy
- Review shows your progress

## Keeping It Going

- Set a fixed time each day
- Keep a note of useful questions
- Share your favourite tips
### The rule
- A little every day
- Save what works
- Teach someone else

## The Week Plan Prompt

```text
Make me a simple 7-day plan to learn AI as a homemaker, with
one small task per day and the exact question to ask each
day. Keep it easy and encouraging.
```
- One task per day
- The exact question to ask
- Encouraging and simple

## Key Takeaways

- Use AI a little every day to build the habit
- Follow a simple seven-day plan
- Try one new small task each day
- Keep a note of questions that worked
- Review your progress and share your tips

## Practice Challenge

1. Write your seven-day plan

3. Keep notes of what worked

2. How many tasks per day?

3. What should you keep?
