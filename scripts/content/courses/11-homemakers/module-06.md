### Module 1 · You Can Teach Others

Once you are comfortable with AI, you can help other women in your community learn it too. This is the AI Didi idea: one woman teaches another.

Teaching helps others and makes your own knowledge stronger.

## Learning Objectives

- See yourself as a teacher
- Understand the AI Didi idea
- Help your community
- Strengthen your own learning

## The AI Didi Idea

- One woman teaches another
- Knowledge spreads through the community
- Everyone grows together
### Why
- You already know enough to start
- Teaching helps others earn and save
- Sharing builds confidence

## Why You Can Teach

- You do not need to know everything
- You only need to be one step ahead
- Your everyday examples help others
### The rule
- Teach what you know
- Use simple examples
- Learn together

## The Teaching Prompt

```text
I am comfortable with AI and want to teach other women in
my community. Suggest how I can start, what to teach first,
and how to keep it simple and friendly.
```
- How to start teaching
- What to teach first
- A friendly approach

## Key Takeaways

- You can teach AI to others in your community
- The AI Didi idea is one woman teaching another
- You only need to be one step ahead
- Teaching strengthens your own knowledge
- Simple everyday examples help others learn

## Practice Challenge

1. Pick one AI skill you know well
2. Think of someone who would benefit
3. Plan to show them this week

**Answer:** A — AI Didi means one woman teaching another in her community.

- A) One step ahead

**Answer:** A — You only need to be one step ahead to help someone else learn.

3. What does teaching do for you?
- A) Strengthens your knowledge
- B) Wastes time
- C) Confuses you
- D) Nothing

**Answer:** A — Teaching others reinforces and strengthens your own learning.

4. What examples help?
- A) Simple everyday ones

- D) Complex ones

**Answer:** A — Simple, everyday examples help your community understand AI.

### Module 3 · Step 2: Make a Simple Lesson Plan

A simple lesson plan keeps your teaching clear: what you will show, how you will show it, and how the learner will practise.

AI can help you plan the lesson in minutes.

## Learning Objectives

- Make a simple lesson plan
- Show, do, and practise
- Keep lessons short and clear
- Plan with AI

## The Lesson Plan

- What you will teach
- How you will show it
- How the learner will practise
### Why
- A plan keeps the lesson clear
- Practice makes learning stick
- Short lessons are easier to follow

## Planning Well

- Keep each lesson short
- Use real examples from daily life
- End with hands-on practice
### The rule
- Show, then let them do
- Keep it short
- Practise together

## The Lesson-Plan Prompt

```text
Make a simple 20-minute lesson plan to teach [skill] to a
beginner homemaker. Include what to show, a real-life
example, and a practice activity. Keep it friendly.
```

- A 20-minute plan
- A real-life example
- A practice activity

## Key Takeaways

- A simple lesson plan keeps teaching clear
- Include what to show, an example, and practice
- Keep lessons short and friendly
- Use real examples from daily life
- End with hands-on practice

**Answer:** A — Short, friendly lessons are easier for beginners to follow.

### Module 4 · Step 3: Teach Your Neighbours

Start with a small group you are comfortable with: neighbours, friends, or relatives. A friendly, hands-on session is the best first step.

Keep it relaxed, practical, and encouraging.

## Learning Objectives

- Teach your first small session
- Keep it friendly and practical
- Encourage questions
- Build confidence in learners

## Your First Session

- Invite a few neighbours
- Show one simple skill
- Let everyone try it on their phone
### Why
- Small groups feel safe
- Hands-on learning works best
- Encouragement keeps learners going

## Teaching Well

- Go slowly and repeat
- Welcome every question
- Celebrate small wins
### The rule
- Practical, not theory
- Patient and encouraging
- Everyone practises

## The Session Prompt

```text
Plan a friendly first AI session for 4-5 neighbours. Pick
one simple skill, list the steps to show, and a practice
activity for everyone. Keep it encouraging.
```
- One simple skill
- Steps to demonstrate
- A group practice activity

## Key Takeaways

- Start with a small, comfortable group
- Teach one simple skill per session
- Keep it hands-on and practical
- Welcome every question patiently
- Celebrate small wins to build confidence

## Practice Challenge

1. Invite three neighbours
2. Plan one simple skill to teach
3. Run the session and note what worked

**Example Quiz**

- A) Neighbours and friends
- B) A huge crowd
- C) Strangers
- D) Nobody

**Answer:** A — Start with a small, comfortable group like neighbours or friends.

2. How many skills per session?
- A) One simple skill
- B) Ten
- C) None
- D) Everything

**Answer:** A — Teach one simple skill per session so it stays clear.

3. What kind of learning works best?
- A) Hands-on practice
- B) Long lectures
- C) Reading
- D) Watching

**Answer:** A — Hands-on practice works best for beginners learning AI.

4. How should you treat questions?
- A) Welcome them patiently
- B) Ignore them
- C) Rush them
- D) Discourage them

**Answer:** A — Welcome every question patiently to keep learners encouraged.

### Module 5 · Step 4: Build a Small Learning Circle

A learning circle is a small group that meets regularly to learn AI together. It keeps everyone motivated and growing.

Over time, the circle can teach even more women in your community.

## Learning Objectives

- Start a small learning circle
- Meet regularly
- Keep everyone motivated
- Grow the circle

## The Learning Circle

- A small group meeting regularly
- Everyone learns and shares
- Each member can teach another
### Why
- Groups keep motivation high
- Shared learning is faster
- The circle grows by itself

## Keeping It Going

- Meet at a fixed time each week
- Rotate who teaches what
- Share success stories
### The rule
- Meet regularly
- Everyone teaches something
- Celebrate progress

## The Circle Prompt

```text
Help me start a small AI learning circle in my community: a
weekly meeting plan, what each member can teach, and how to
keep everyone motivated and growing.
```
- A weekly plan
- What each member teaches
- Motivation ideas

## Key Takeaways

- A learning circle is a small group meeting regularly
- Regular meetings keep everyone motivated
- Members teach and learn from each other
- Rotate who teaches to share knowledge
- The circle can grow to reach more women

## Practice Challenge

1. Invite four women to a circle
2. Set a fixed weekly time
3. Plan who teaches next week

**Example Quiz**

1. What is a learning circle?
- A) A small group meeting regularly
- B) A big class
- C) A website
- D) A paid course

**Answer:** A — A learning circle is a small group that meets regularly to learn.

2. What keeps motivation high?
- A) Regular meetings
- B) Meeting once
- C) No meetings
- D) Silence

**Answer:** A — Meeting regularly keeps everyone motivated and growing.

3. Who teaches in the circle?
- A) Everyone takes turns
- B) Only one person
- C) Nobody
- D) A stranger

**Answer:** A — Rotating who teaches shares knowledge across the whole group.

4. What can the circle do over time?
- A) Grow to reach more women
- B) Shrink to nothing
- C) Stay the same forever
- D) Close

**Answer:** A — A healthy circle grows and reaches more women in the community.

AI Didis do not just learn - they lift the whole community with them.

## Learning Objectives

- See your full AI journey
- Plan your next steps
- Keep learning and earning
- Lift your community

## Your Journey

- **Learn:** get comfortable with AI
- **Use:** apply it daily at home
- **Earn:** start a micro-business or freelance
- **Teach:** help other women learn
### Why
- A roadmap keeps you moving
- Each step builds on the last
- Your growth lifts others

## Planning Ahead

- Set one goal for each step
- Keep practising daily
- Teach as you grow
### The rule
- Learn, use, earn, teach
- One goal at a time
- Bring others with you
