### Module 1 · Earning from Home with AI

Freelancing means doing small paid jobs from home, on your own time. With AI, you can do many of these jobs faster and better.

This mission shows you the kinds of gigs you can do and how AI helps you earn.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand what freelancing is
- See the kinds of gigs available
- Use AI to work faster
- Start earning from home

## Freelancing Basics

- Small paid jobs done from home
- You choose your hours
- Many kinds of work available
### Why
- It fits around home duties
- You can start with little equipment
- Skills matter more than degrees

## Kinds of Gigs

- Writing and translating
- Data entry and simple admin
- Social media help for small shops
- Transcription and subtitles
- Designing simple posters
### The rule
- Pick gigs that fit your skills
- Start with one kind
- Use AI to work faster

## The Freelance Prompt

```text
I want to earn from home as a freelancer. My skills are
[list] and I can work [hours] a day. Suggest 5 freelance
gigs that fit me and how AI can help with each.
```
- Five fitting gigs
- How AI helps each
- Suited to your hours

## Key Takeaways

- Freelancing means small paid jobs from home
- You choose your own hours
- Gigs include writing, translation, admin, and design
- Skills matter more than degrees
- AI helps you work faster and better

## Practice Challenge

1. List your skills and available hours
2. Ask AI for five gig ideas
3. Pick one to try

### Module 5 · Step 4: Getting Paid Safely

Earning is only useful if you get paid. Learn safe ways to receive money and protect yourself from cheats.

This lesson covers simple payment safety for freelancers.

## Learning Objectives

- Receive payments safely
- Spot payment scams
- Agree terms clearly
- Protect your earnings

## Safe Payment

- Agree the price and payment method first
- Use trusted payment apps
- Keep a record of every payment
### Why
- Clear terms prevent disputes
- Trusted apps are safer
- Records protect you

## Spotting Scams

- No OTP is ever needed to receive money
- Beware of pay-a-fee-to-earn offers
- Do not share bank details carelessly
### The rule
- Agree terms in writing
- Never share OTPs
- Use trusted apps only

## The Payment Prompt

```text
Explain safe ways for a freelancer to receive payments, how
to spot payment scams, and a simple message to agree price
and payment terms with a client.
```
- Safe payment methods
- Scam warning signs
- A terms message

## Key Takeaways

- Agree the price and payment method before starting
- Use trusted payment apps and keep records
- No OTP is ever needed to receive money
- Beware of pay-a-fee-to-earn offers
- Never share bank details or OTPs carelessly

## Practice Challenge

1. Write a payment terms message
2. List three scam warning signs
3. Set up a safe payment app

**Example Quiz**

1. What should you agree first?
- A) Price and payment method
- B) Nothing
- C) Only the deadline
- D) The weather

**Answer:** A — Agree the price and payment method before you start work.

2. When do you share an OTP?
- A) Never
- B) Always
- C) When asked
- D) For payment

**Answer:** A — No OTP is ever needed to receive money - never share it.

3. What is a scam sign?
- A) Pay a fee to earn
- B) A clear contract
- C) A trusted app
- D) A receipt

**Answer:** A — Being asked to pay a fee to earn money is a classic scam.

4. What should you keep?
- A) A record of payments
- B) Nothing
- C) Only cash
- D) Only promises

**Answer:** A — Keep a record of every payment to protect your earnings.

### Module 6 · Step 5: Building Repeat Clients

One-time gigs pay once; repeat clients pay again and again. Good service and clear communication turn clients into regulars.

AI helps you stay in touch politely and offer more value over time.

## Learning Objectives

- Turn clients into regulars
- Communicate well between jobs
- Offer more value
- Grow steady income

## Keeping Clients

- Deliver quality every time
- Reply politely and promptly
- Check in when there is new work
### Why
- Repeat clients are steady income
- They recommend you to others
- Trust grows with each job

## Offering More

- Ask if they need related work
- Offer a small discount for regulars
- Keep a list of your clients
### The rule
- Great service first
- Stay in polite touch
- Grow steadily

## The Repeat-Client Prompt

```text
Write a polite message to follow up with a past client, ask
if they have new work, and mention related services I offer.
Keep it warm and not pushy.
```
- A polite follow-up
- Related services mentioned
- Warm and not pushy

## Key Takeaways

- Repeat clients provide steady income
- Deliver quality every time to earn repeat work
- Reply politely and promptly
- Check in when there may be new work
- Offer related services to grow your income
