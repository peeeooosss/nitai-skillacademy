# Mission 3: AI for Everyday Life — Cooking, Kids, Home, Money

Now that you can ask well, apply AI across the four pillars of home life. This mission is a toolbox of ready-to-use prompts you can save and reuse forever.

## Learning Objectives

- Use AI to plan meals, markets lists, and budget-friendly menus.
- Use AI to help with children's studies and family communication.
- Use AI to organise the home: cleaning plans, travel packing, important dates.
- Use AI to understand money: budgets, small savings plans, and financial basics.
- Create your personal "prompt notebook" of prompts you will reuse.

## The Core Idea

AI is most powerful when it becomes a daily habit, not a one-time try. The trick is to prepare your best prompts once, save them, and reuse them whenever the situation repeats. This mission gives you the ready-made starter set.

### Cooking & Food

- **Menu planning:** "Plan a 7-day dinner menu for a family of 4 on a medium budget. Two vegetarian days, two non-vegetarian days, one egg day, one leftovers day, one light day. Give a market list separately."
- **Leftover rescue:** "I have [list ingredients]. What can I make for dinner tonight?"
- **Kid-friendly:** "Suggest a meal that uses green vegetables but a 6-year-old will enjoy eating."
- **Health needs:** "Plan a diabetic-friendly week of meals" — but always confirm any health information with a doctor.

### Children's Studies

- **Explain it fun:** "Explain fractions to a 9-year-old using a pizza."
- **Exam revision:** "Make 10 practice questions on [topic] for a class-5 student."
- **Project help:** "Help my child make a school project on water conservation. Give simple points, a small poem, and a drawing idea."
- **Letters:** "Write a note to my child's teacher asking for a meeting about my son's reading progress."

### Home & Organisation

- **Cleaning:** "Make a simple 7-day home cleaning checklist for a 3-room house. One hour per day maximum."
- **Travel:** "Pack a list for a 3-day trip with a baby, in a small car, in summer."
- **Important dates:** "Create a list of things to remember before every festival — cleaning, shopping, guests, sweets."
- **Festivals:** "Plan a budget for Diwali for a family of 4, decorating, gifts, sweets, and new clothes included."

### Money Understanding

Never follow money advice blindly — AI is a helper, and bank rules change. Use it to *understand* and *plan*, then confirm with official sources.

- **Budget:** "Build a monthly budget table for a family of 4. Income 25,000 rupees. Food, rent, school, transport, savings. Show me what can be saved."
- **Savings:** "Explain a fixed deposit vs a recurring deposit in very simple words. Which suits a homemaker saving 2,000 rupees a month?"
- **School fees:** "Help me plan savings for my daughter's annual school fees of 12,000 rupees, due in 10 months."

### The "What If" Question

The most useful habit you will ever build: whenever you face a decision, ask AI *"What are my options and their pros and cons?"* — for a purchase, a school choice, a health question to raise with the doctor, a family decision. It gives you a structured view to think with.

## Real-World Examples

**Example 1 – Sunday planning.** Meera now spends Sunday morning with her AI app: she plans the week's menu (Mission 3 prompts), gets the market list, and checks which days she can double-cook. Mondays are calmer; food waste is down.

**Example 2 – The exam week.** During her daughter's exams, Sunita asked AI to: make 10 practice questions on each subject, explain each wrong answer simply, and create a study timetable. Her daughter practised more calmly and her stress dropped.

**Example 3 – The emergency month.** When an unexpected medical expense arrived, Lakshmi asked AI for a revised one-month budget with the emergency included, and where she could trim first. She had a plan within ten minutes instead of a sleepless night of worry.

## Key Takeaways

- AI works best as a daily habit: plan the menu, the week, the budget, the homework help.
- Save your best prompts in a notebook (paper or phone) — the same prompt works every time.
- For every significant decision, ask AI "What are my options and pros and cons?"
- Health finance and legal answers always need a human expert's confirmation.
- The effort is one-time; the time saved is every single week.
