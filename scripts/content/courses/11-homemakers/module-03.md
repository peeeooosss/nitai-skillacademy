### Module 1 · Your Everyday Helper

This mission is about putting AI to work in your daily life: in the kitchen, with the children, around the home, and with money.

Small daily uses add up to real time saved and less stress.

## Learning Objectives

- Use AI across daily life
- Save time and reduce stress
- Pick uses that fit your day
- Build confidence with practice

## Where It Helps

- Cooking and meal planning
- Children and homework
- Home tasks and errands
- Money and budgets
### Why
- These tasks repeat daily
- AI gives quick, useful help
- Small savings add up

## Using It Daily

- Pick the task you dislike most
- Ask AI to help with it first
- Use it again tomorrow
### The rule
- Start with what stresses you
- Use it a little every day
- Keep what works

## The Everyday Prompt

```text
Help me use AI in my daily life. Here is my routine [list].
Suggest the 4 tasks AI can help most with and a simple
question to ask for each.
```
- Four best tasks
- A question for each
- Simple and practical

## Key Takeaways

- AI can help with cooking, kids, home, and money
- These are tasks that repeat every day
- Start with the task that stresses you most
- Use AI a little every day
- Small daily savings add up

## Practice Challenge

1. Write your daily routine
2. Mark the four most tiring tasks
3. Ask AI to help with one today

### Module 3 · Step 2: Helping Your Kids Learn

AI can help your children with homework, explain difficult topics in simple words, and create practice questions.

You stay involved, using AI as a patient tutor that never runs out of time.

## Learning Objectives

- Help kids with homework
- Explain topics simply
- Create practice questions
- Stay involved in learning

## Learning with AI

- Explain a topic simply
- Give practice questions
- Check homework answers
- Tell stories and rhymes
### Why
- AI is patient and endless
- Simple explanations help children
- Practice builds confidence

## Using It Well

- Ask for the child's age and class
- Ask for examples a child understands
- Do not let AI replace the child's own work
### The rule
- Explain, do not just answer
- Match the child's age
- The child still learns

## The Learning Prompt

```text

words with one everyday example. Then give 5 practice
questions with answers.
```
- A simple explanation
- An everyday example
- Five practice questions

## Key Takeaways

- AI explains topics in simple words for children
- It creates practice questions and checks answers
- Match the explanation to the child's age
- AI should explain, not just give answers
- The child must still do their own learning

### Module 5 · Step 4: Managing Money and Budgets

AI can help you make a simple household budget, track expenses, and find ways to save. It explains money matters in plain words.

It is a helper for planning, not a replacement for a financial adviser.

## Learning Objectives

- Make a simple household budget
- Track expenses
- Find ways to save
- Understand money in plain words

## Money with AI

- A simple monthly budget
- Tracking where money goes
- Ideas to save on bills and groceries
- Explaining savings and loans simply
### Why
- A budget shows where money goes
- Small savings add up
- Plain explanations help you decide

## Using It Safely

- Use amounts and categories, not account numbers
- Never share OTPs or passwords
- Ask a real adviser for big decisions
### The rule
- Budget with categories
- Never share bank details
- Check big decisions with a person

## The Budget Prompt

```text
Help me make a simple monthly household budget. My income
is [amount] and my main expenses are [list]. Show a simple
table and 3 ways to save. Do not ask for bank details.
```
- A simple budget table
- Three ways to save
- No bank details needed

## Key Takeaways

- AI helps make a simple household budget
- It tracks expenses and suggests savings
- Use amounts and categories, never account numbers
- Never share OTPs or passwords
- Ask a real adviser for big money decisions

## Practice Challenge

4. Who should you ask for big decisions?

### Module 6 · Step 5: Health, Wellbeing, and Family Care

AI can give general information on health, fitness, and caring for family, and help you prepare questions for a doctor.

But it is not a doctor. For any real health problem, always see a qualified professional.

## Learning Objectives

- Get general health information
- Prepare questions for the doctor
- Support family wellbeing
- Know AI is not a doctor

## Health and Care

- General information on common issues
- Simple fitness and diet ideas
- Tips for caring for elders and children
- Questions to ask your doctor
### Why
- Information helps you prepare
- Wellbeing habits support the family
- A doctor must decide on treatment

## Staying Safe

- Never use AI to diagnose or prescribe
- Always see a doctor for real problems
- Check anything serious with a professional
### The rule
- General info only
- Doctor for real problems
- Emergencies: call for help

## The Health Prompt

```text
Give me general information about [common issue] in simple
words, and 5 questions I should ask my doctor about it.
Remind me this is not medical advice.
```
- General information
- Questions for the doctor
- A safety reminder

## Key Takeaways

- AI gives general health information only
- It helps you prepare questions for the doctor
- It can suggest simple wellbeing habits
- AI is not a doctor and must not diagnose or prescribe
- For any real problem, see a qualified professional
