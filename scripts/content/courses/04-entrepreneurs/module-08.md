# Mission 8: Launch Plan, Metrics & First Customers

## Learning Objectives
- Design a 14-day launch that converts contacts into customers
- Define the 5 numbers that matter (and ignore vanity dashboards)
- Win your first 3 paying customers with a repeatable sales loop
- Decide the next chapter from evidence — run, fix, or pivot
- Build the founder dashboard that keeps you honest weekly

---

## Launch Isn't an Event — It's a Campaign

Entrepreneurs wait for "launch day" hype that never comes. A good launch is a **14-day campaign of direct, warm, targeted offers** — not one social post. By the end of this mission you'll have first customers or clear evidence about what to fix.

## Step 1: The 14-Day Launch Campaign

Structure (borrow from Mission 5's playbook but aimed at buyers, not investors):

- **Days 1–2:** tell your Mission 2 contacts the product is live (they already know the pain; give them the demo link + a first-customer offer).
- **Days 3–10:** daily outreach — one warm, specific, non-spammy message per contact. Follow up on no-replies by day 7 with one nudge (Mission 5 rule).
- **Days 5–14:** publish proof: sample output, the 60-second demo, one mini-essay "we built X for Y and here's the result." Cross-post your best content twice (platforms die fast).
- **All through:** collect every reply into your tracker and keep the sales loop honest (below).

> "Design my 14-day launch for [offer] to [segment]: day-by-day sequence, offer structure (early-customer discount? launch price?), and the exact scripts for day-1 announce, daily outreach, and the one follow-up. Keep it 30-min-a-day effort."

## Step 2: The 5 Numbers That Matter

Ignore the dashboard-shiny; track the 5 that decide your future:

1. **Conversations per week** (real chats with potential buyers)
2. **Pipeline send** (offers/products pitched)
3. **Conversion** (offers → paying customers)
4. **Repeatable revenue** (MRR or money-in-30-days for service)
5. **Delivery cost per customer** (time + tools per fulfilled deal — your Mission 3 unit economics, updated)

Vanity to skip for now: social followers, "impressions," signups who never paid, and the 1001 metrics your tool vendor yells about.

> "Given my tracker data, define how I'll count these 5 numbers weekly and what step-change (not a 5% wiggle) should make me change course."

## Step 3: The Repeatable Sales Loop (First 3 Customers)

Sales before you feel "ready":

- **Offer something small and real first** — a diagnostic, a done-for-you deliverable, a starter plan. Small offers earn trust and feedback fast.
- **Never chase churn-proof infinity:** 3 people who paid → you learn delivery, pricing, objection handling, and channel truth (Mission 3's unit economics get real numbers).
- **The loop, repeated:** talk to N (#1) → pitch → close → deliver → collect feedback/🏅 proof → repeat with the contact's network. A founding customer asks a friend — that's your first sales channel.

> "Write my first-customer sales script: opening that references their pain, the small offer, handling 'I'll think about it' and 'too expensive' with honest answers, and the closing ask. 90 seconds, human, no hard sell."

## Step 4: The Evidence Meeting (Fork in the Road)

After the 14 days + ~10 conversations, decide with data:

- **RUN:** customers paying, delivery manageable, revised unit economics positive → increase the channel (Mission 6), add pricing tier, go full-time on the loop.
- **FIX:** interest but no sales → change price/offer/channel (change one thing; retest 14 days). Most first launches need a fix, not a pivot.
- **PIVOT:** customers give you a different, better problem than your idea → follow the evidence to the adjacent problem (validated by real behaviour now).

**How to know you're lying to yourself:** if you keep collecting "soft interest" (likes, "sounds cool") but zero payment conversations, that's not validation — that's a preference for comfort. Paid is the only signal that counts.

## Step 5: The Weekly Founder Dashboard

After launch, maintain a 30-minute Friday ritual:

- Update the 5 numbers.
- Ask: "Where did I convert, where did I stall, and what's the ONE thing to improve next week?"
- Log the week in your brag file (wins, rejections, lessons).

## Key Takeaways

- Launch = a 14-day campaign of warm, targeted offers, not one hype post.
- Track 5 numbers: conversations, sends, conversion, revenue, delivery cost — skip vanity metrics.
- Win 3 paying customers with small real offers; the loop is talk → pitch → deliver → reference.
- Decide RUN / FIX / PIVOT on evidence; paid behaviour is the only signal.
- Keep a 30-min Friday founder ritual: 5 numbers + one improvement.

## Practice Challenge (Final)

1. **Run the 14-day campaign** — launch your live offer (you built it in Mission 4), with scripts and daily 30-min effort.
2. **Count the 5 numbers** weekly for 14 days. No vanity metrics in your tracker.
3. **Close your first 3 customers.** Deliver, collect their proof, and ask each for one referral.
4. **Hold the evidence meeting:** RUN / FIX / PIVOT decision in writing with 3 reasons.
5. **Commit to the ritual:** schedule your Friday 30-minute dashboard and write Week 1's entry now — including the ONE improvement for next week. 🚀
