# Mission 1: Idea Hunt & AI Trend Research

## Learning Objectives
- Generate a wide pool of startup ideas systematically
- Use AI to validate market timing and trend direction
- Filter ideas by 4 killer criteria before falling in love
- Skip the "vanity idea" traps founders fall into
- Choose ONE idea to take into Mission 2

---

## The Founder's First Job: Choose the Right Problem

Most failed startups didn't die from bad execution — they died from **unvalidated ideas**. Your first mission is deliberately boring: build a wide pool, filter it hard, pick one thing worth your next 90 days.

## Generate a Wide Pool (Quantity First)

Ideas are a numbers game. Generate across categories to avoid your own bias:

> "Act as a startup strategist. Given my skills [X], interests [Y], and region [Z], generate 20 business ideas in 5 batches: local services, B2B tools, AI-native content, e-commerce niches, and skill-based offerings. For each: one-line pitch and who it serves."

Then **add your unfair advantage filter**: which 5 use skills, networks, or resources YOU already have? Ideas you're uniquely suited for beat objectively "better" ideas you can't win.

## The 4-Filter Screen (Kill Bad Ideas Fast)

For each idea, ask AI to score it, then apply your judgment:

1. **Pain?** Does someone feel this problem weekly? (Not "nice to have.")
2. **Pays?** Will someone actually pay money? (Free users aren't customers.)
3. **Reachable?** Can you reach/price your customer cheaply (local market, platform, community)?
4. **Timely?** Is the market accelerating? (AI tools, remote work, regulatory tailwinds...)

> "Score these 5 ideas against: pain (1-5), willingness-to-pay (1-5), reachability in my market (1-5), timing/trend (1-5). Flag strengths and blind spots for each."

**Red-flag ideas to skip:** "I had this dream" with zero customer contact; "Uber for X" with no distribution reason; "AI wrapper with no moat" with no special access; "I'll figure out the market later" (no — market first).

## Read the Trend, Not the Hype

AI trend research done right:

- **Primary sources first:** marketplaces, job boards (what employers pay for = what businesses need), regulator pages, and real communities — not AI summaries of hype.
- **Ask for verifiable signals:** "What signals indicate [trend] is growing: job posts with 'AI,' spending reports from [published sources], platform API usage trends? Which are verifiable vs opinion?"

Classify every signal: **evidence** (I can open the source) vs **hype** (AI's confident guess).

## Pick Your ONE Idea (FOMO-Proof)

Choose your single idea for Mission 2 using this test:

> **"If I get zero results for 60 days, would I still enjoy working on this?"** — enjoyment is your only guaranteed runway.

Write your one-liner: *"I'm building [X] for [customer] because [pain], and I'll profit via [pricing]."* Print it. Every mission after this serves that sentence.

## The 90-Day Founder Runway

Commit to a focused sprint (the rest of this course):

- **Weeks 1–2:** validate the idea (Mission 2) with real conversations.
- **Weeks 3–4:** design the business model (Mission 3) and build a landing/MVP shell (Mission 4).
- **Weeks 5–6:** launch + get first users + pitch materials (Missions 5–6).
- **Weeks 7–8:** systems, metrics, and first revenue (Missions 7–8).

If at week 4 evidence says "no," you pivot the idea, not the method — the skills transfer.

## Key Takeaways

- Ideas are a numbers game: generate wide, then filter by unfair advantage.
- Score with 4 criteria: Pain, Pays, Reachable, Timely.
- Read real signals (job posts, marketplaces, spending) over AI hype.
- Choose one idea you'd enjoy for 60 days with nothing to show; write the one-liner.
- Commit to a 90-day runway with validate → build → launch → systemise.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Generate 20 ideas** across the 5 batches above. Shortlist 5 that use your unfair advantage.
2. **Score them** with the 4-filter prompt; apply your own judgment to each score.
3. **Signal check:** pick 2 trends from your ideas and produce 5 verifiable signals for each (open at least 3 sources). Separate evidence from hype in your notes.
4. **Pick your ONE:** write your one-liner and print it. Post it where you'll see it daily for the next 90 days.
