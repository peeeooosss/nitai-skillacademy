# Mission 4: MVP & Landing Page with AI

## Learning Objectives
- Scope an MVP that validates, not impresses
- Build a landing page that converts in a weekend with AI
- Wire a lead/pre-order capture signal
- Create your first demo video without production skills
- Decide build vs no-code for your core product

---

## The MVP Trap: Overbuilding Is the Real Failure

An MVP's job isn't to be impressive — it's to **get evidence** (pre-orders, sign-ups, conversations). The leanest MVP you can defend earns the same evidence as the fancy one, in a fraction of the time.

**MVP scope test:** can you deliver value to 3 paying/committed users within 14 days? If not, cut scope.

## Step 1: Scope the Leanest Version

Take your core value (Mission 3) and cut it:

**The 3-part MVP recipe:**
1. The **minimum** that solves the core problem for 1 user type.
2. The **ugliect** possible version of it (manual behind the curtain counts!).
3. One **obvious next** step once used.

> "Take my one-pager [paste]. Propose the smallest 14-day MVP: what to include, what to explicitly NOT build, and what a manual/human-in-the-middle version would look like. Make it ugly on purpose."

**Wizard of Oz MVP:** pretend it's automated; you secretly do the steps manually. (Doing it manually for 3 users is a feature — it teaches you the real workflow.)

## Step 2: The Converting Landing Page (Weekend Build)

One page must answer 3 questions in 30 seconds:

1. **What is it?** (headline + one sentence)
2. **Who's it for + what's the result?** (pain → promise)
3. **Why now / why you?** (proof, specifics, and a clear CALL TO ACTION)

Structure (copy-paste classic):
- Hero: headline + subhead + CTA button
- Problem → Solution (in customer's own words from Mission 2)
- How it works (3 steps)
- Social proof you have (testimonials, numbers, logos even if small)
- Pricing (from Mission 3)
- FAQ (answer objections)
- Final CTA + contact

**AI copy sprint:**
> "Write landing page copy for [solution] to [customer] with pain [paste quotes]. Sections: hero, problem→solution, how it works (3 steps), proof, pricing, FAQ. Tone: [confident/helpful]. 25 words per section max. Use my customer quotes verbatim where possible."

Then **you edit** — generic AI copy reads generic. Inject your voice and your real proof.

**Build fast:** no-code (Cardd/Framer/Notion site) or a template — speed beats hand-rolled. Host free (Netlify/Vercel).

## Step 3: Capture the Signal (Lead, Pre-Order, or Waitlist)

The page is useless without collection:

- **Waitlist + pre-order tier:** "Reserve with ₹X (refundable)" if you can take money; otherwise strong waitlist with stated launch date.
- **Single form field max** (email). Friction kills conversion.
- **Post-signup:** send an instant honest confirmation: "You're #N — we're building X, launch [date]."

That signal list is your Mission 2 evidence, now with real behaviour behind it.

## Step 4: The 60-Second Demo (No Editing Skills Needed)

Don't ship no demo. A 60-second Loom/screen record: problem in 15s → show the ugly product solving it in 30s → result + CTA in 15s.

- Script it in 4 lines (use AI), then speak naturally.
- Record the screen + your face if comfortable — trust compounds with faces.
- Add one caption per section so silent-viewers get it.

## Step 5: Build vs No-Code Core

For the actual product, choose pragmatically:

| Approach | When |
|---|---|
| **No-code glue** (sheet, forms, automation, templates) | Validating — fastest, cheapest |
| **Framework starter** (Next/Vite + AI coding help) | Real product urgency + you can code |
| **AI-agent tiny product** (prompted workflow / custom GPT) | Pure value gate, no heavy UI |

> "Given my MVP scope, rank no-code vs coded vs agent-based for MY core: effort, cost, speed to 3 users, and long-term ceiling."

**Golden rule:** the MVP that ships on Friday beats the perfect one arriving in a month.

## Key Takeaways

- MVP = minimum + ugly + one next step; humans-in-the-loop are allowed.
- Landing page answers what / who / why-now in 30 seconds; AI drafts, you edit.
- Capture every visitor (waitlist/pre-order) — that's your validation data.
- Ship a 60-second demo: problem → ugly solution → result → CTA.
- Choose build/no-code/agent by effort and speed to your first 3 users.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Write the MVP scope** (14-day, ugly version) — share it, cut it further.
2. **Launch the landing page** with a waitlist/pre-order capture and honest confirmation email. Aim: within 48 hours.
3. **Collect signal:** get 5+ real sign-ups/leads and document what they said.
4. **Record the 60-second demo** (4-line script, captions) and put it above the fold or in your submission.
5. **Decision memo:** choose build/no-code/agent and justify in 3 lines.
