# Mission 5: Pitch Deck & Investor Materials

## Learning Objectives
- Write a founder story that opens decks with emotion and credibility
- Structure a 10-slide pitch deck investors can scan in minutes
- Build financial projections that are honest, not fantasy hockey sticks
- Prepare your one-pager and demo assets every meeting needs
- Practise the 60-second and 6-minute pitch with AI

---

## Pitch Deck Purpose: Make Them Want the Next Call

A deck doesn't raise money by itself — it earns the next meeting. Investors decide in the first minutes whether to keep reading. Your deck's real readers are (1) the founder's story, (2) proof of demand, (3) honest numbers, (4) a clear ask.

## The 10-Slide Standard (Scan the Funnel)

1. **Title:** name + one-line thesis + "we're raising [X]".
2. **Problem:** the pain in the customer's own words (Mission 2 quotes = gold).
3. **Solution:** how your product fixes it; one demo screen.
4. **Why us / market timing:** the trend window (Mission 1 evidence).
5. **Traction:** real proof — users, revenue, waitlist, LOIs. Not projections.
6. **Business model & unit economics** (Mission 3 numbers).
7. **Market size:** top-down + bottom-up (bottom-up wins trust).
8. **Team:** why YOU uniquely can win (unfair advantage section).
9. **Financial plan & ask:** honest 18-month forecast + what funding buys + milestones.
10. **Contact:** simple, clean, one CTA.

> "Given my one-pager and validation notes [paste], draft a skeleton for this 10-slide deck. For each slide give: headline, 3 bullets max, and 1 question the slide must answer. Keep it skimmable — investors scan."

## The Founder Story That Hooks

Before structure, nail the story: investors fund people first. Use the arc:

- **Origin:** the moment you saw the problem with your own eyes (specific > grand).
- **Obsession:** why this problem won't let go of you.
- **Tension:** what's hard or why most people haven't solved it.
- **Resolution path:** why YOU are equipped (skills, access, unfair advantage from Mission 1).

> "Draft my 2-minute founder story using my background [paste]: origin, obsession, tension, path. End with the mission sentence. Keep it first-person and honest."

## Projections That Don't Look Painted

Investors have seen 100 straight-line hockey sticks to $100M. Yours must feel earned:

- **Bottom-up build:** price × realistic conversion × reachable leads — literally arithmetic, not vibes.
- **Three scenarios:** conservative (survival), base (plan), optimistic (if X happens).
- **Show the assumptions** next to the numbers: "assuming 2% visit→pay conversion."
- **Match the ask to the milestone:** raise to reach the NEXT evidence point (traction + team), not to "change the world."

> "Draft 18-month projections (conservative/base/optimistic) from my pricing + validation data. Show the assumptions for each line. Death-spiral the base case if revenue stays at today's level."

## The Meeting Kit (Have These Ready)

Every investor call needs:

- **One-pager (1 page):** story + problem + traction + ask. (AI helps draft; you make it earn trust.)
- **Deck in a 3-minute version:** cut 10 slides to the 6 that matter.
- **The demo:** your Mission 4 video/demo — 60 seconds, rehearsed.
- **The backup materials:** customer quotes, pipeline, LOIs, metrics definitions.

> "Turn my deck into a 6-slide '3-minute version.' Cut ruthlessly: which slides prove the story, which can live in the appendix?"

## Practise the Two Pitches

- **The 60-second** (lift-pitch, networking): problem → solution → one proof → ask. Every founder needs it at zero notice.
- **The 6-minute** (meeting): full story with 2 well-policed proofs points and a crisp ask.

AI practice loop (borrowed from interview missions):
> "Act as a blunt investor. Ask me 5 tough questions about my deck: traction, churn, moat, competition, and why me. Score my answers 1–5 and push twice on my weakest."

Record yourself. The goal isn't polish; it's that you know your numbers cold — an investor who pokes a hole in a number you can defend from memory respect it.

## Key Takeaways

- The deck's job: earn the next call, not close on slide one.
- 10 slides: problem → solution → timing → traction → model → size → team → plan/ask.
- Lead with the founder story: origin, obsession, tension, path.
- Projections are bottom-up three-scenario arithmetic with visible assumptions.
- Prepare the one-pager, 3-minute deck, 60-sec demo, and back-up metrics — practise with a blunt AI investor.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Write your founder story** (AI-drafted, rewritten by you) and rehearse it aloud 3 times.
2. **Build the 10-slide deck** skeleton with your real numbers (no hypothetical traction).
3. **Projections:** build the 3-scenario model with assumptions visible. Sanity check the conservative case against your cashflow (Mission 3).
4. **Prep the kit:** one-pager + 3-minute deck + your demo + back-up metrics.
5. **Practise:** run one blunt-AI investor Q&A; record your 60-second pitch and refine.
