# Mission 2: Market Validation with AI

## Learning Objectives
- Turn assumptions into testable hypotheses
- Find and interview 10 real target customers
- Build AI-assisted research that guides (never replaces) conversations
- Read demand signals from real search and marketplace data
- Make a clear GO / PIVOT / KILL decision with evidence

---

## Validation = Talking, Not Googling

Desk research can't tell you if people will pay. **Only conversations reveal truth.** This mission's goal: 10 customer conversations + cheap demand signals → a defensible GO/PIVOT/KILL decision.

## Step 1: Turn Assumptions Into Hypotheses

Write 3–5 falsifiable hypotheses from your Mission 1 one-liner:

- **Problem hypothesis:** "My customer, [type], feels [pain] at least once a week."
- **Solution hypothesis:** "They would use [X] if it costs [₹/price] and saves [time/money]."
- **Market hypothesis:** "There are enough of them reachable via [channel] in [region]."

> "Sharpen my hypotheses: for each, what evidence would make it TRUE, and what would prove it FALSE? Make them testable with a 10-interview plan."

## Step 2: Find 10 Real Customers (Not Friends)

- **Nose-nibbling math:** don't handpick friendly people. Find people who genuinely have the problem — through LinkedIn, communities, directories of your target industry, local business groups, suppliers to that industry.
- **Reach-out prompt:**
> "Write a short DM/email asking [customer type] for a 15-minute chat about [pain]. End with a low-commitment ask. No selling. Tone: curious peer, not pitch." Send 30+; expect 10 replies.
- **Snowball:** end every call with "Who else should I talk to?" — one warm intro beats five cold DMs.

## Step 3: The Interview (Listen More Than You Talk)

Rules:
- **90% their story, 10% your idea.** Ask about their week, their workflow, the last time this problem hurt them.
- **Ask for their workarounds:** "What do you do today when this happens?" Workarounds = pain.
- **Ask money questions early:** "What have you already tried or paid for?" (Behaviour, not whether they like X.)
- **Never pitch your solution during validation.** Describe it only if asked, then immediately test with a make-believe: "Would you pay [price]/month for a service that [X]? What would stop you?"

Interview script shortcuts:
> "Give me a 15-minute validation interview script for [customer] and [pain]: opening, 6 questions on current behaviour + money, and 2 'would you pay' probes."

## Step 4: Cheap Demand Signals (Before You Build)

Combine conversations with observed behaviour:

- **Search/marketplace signals:** search volumes (Google Trends relative), how many competing offers exist, and the prices actually charged. "What search and marketplace signals exist for [solution], and what do the price ranges tell me?"
- **Pre-orders over opinions:** a landing page with "pre-pay ₹X to reserve" (Mission 4) is stronger proof than any survey.
- **Competitor reactions:** if incumbents exist, that's validation (there's demand) AND a warning (you must beat them on something specific).

## Step 5: The Go / Pivot / Kill Meeting

After 10 conversations + signals, grade evidence:

| Signal | Strong if... |
|---|---|
| Pain frequency | ≥ 8/10 report the problem actively (not "it would be nice") |
| Money | ≥ 3 have paid for something similar |
| Pre-order | ≥ 5 sign-ups / reservations on your landing page |
| Workaround | Many use manual/fragmented fixes = opportunity |

Decision frameworks:
- **GO:** pain strong + some willingness to pay + reachable channel.
- **PIVOT:** pain real but wrong slice / wrong message or channel. Change who·what·where — not the whole idea.
- **KILL:** customers shrug, no money behaviour, no channel. Let go now; sunk-cost is not a strategy.

Tempting trap: "I'll just build it, maybe people will come." That's how months disappear.

## Key Takeaways

- Validation = conversations + observed behaviour, not Google results.
- Write 3–5 falsifiable hypotheses; define what disproves them in advance.
- Interview 10 real targets (not friends): 90% listening, ask about workarounds and money paid.
- Add cheap demand signals: trends, marketplace prices, pre-orders.
- Decide GO / PIVOT / KILL with a scoreboard and no sunk-cost loyalty.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Write 3 hypotheses** in falsifiable form.
2. **Reach out:** contact 30 real potential customers with your short note; book 10 chats (snowball every one).
3. **Run the interviews** using your script; log every "workaround" and money reference.
4. **Build evidence:** set up a pre-order/reservation promise (you'll launch it properly in Mission 4) and collect 5+ signals.
5. **Decide:** hold your GO/PIVOT/KILL meeting with yourself — write the decision and 3 reasons why.
