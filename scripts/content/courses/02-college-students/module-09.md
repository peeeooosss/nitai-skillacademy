# Mission 9: Portfolio Building for Internships

## Learning Objectives
- Design a portfolio that tells one clear story about you
- Select and frame your best projects with a narrative
- Use AI to polish, structure, and review your portfolio
- Write project case studies in the STAR format
- Make your portfolio easy to find and quick to scan

---

## Your Portfolio Is a Story, Not a Folder

Recruiters spend ~30 seconds on a portfolio before deciding whether to look closer. In 30 seconds they ask one question: *"What can this person do?"* So your portfolio should answer it instantly with a story:

> "I solve [type of problem] with [your tools + AI], and here's proof."

This mission turns your Mission 8 project (and any past work) into that proof.

## The 3-Section Portfolio Plan

**Section 1 — About (2–3 sentences).** "I'm a [year/major] who builds [domain] tools using AI + [skills]. I'm interested in [role type]."

**Section 2 — Projects (2–3, quality over quantity).** Each project shows: problem → what you built → result → what you learned.

**Section 3 — Skills + Contact.** 6–8 real skills, clean and honest (no "expert" on things you've only watched). GitHub, LinkedIn, email.

Choose **2–3 projects max**. Every project must fit your story — a great unrelated project can still be cut if it confuses the message.

## The STAR Case Study Frame

For every project, write a mini case study using **STAR**:

- **S — Situation:** the problem and who had it.
- **T — Task:** what specifically you had to deliver.
- **A — Action:** what you did (tools, AI, approach — be specific).
- **R — Result:** measured where possible + one lesson.

**AI boost:** "Turn my notes [paste] into a STAR case study, 120 words, interview-ready. Lead with the result." Then edit aggressively for honesty — an inflated "Result" is the #1 thing interviewers catch.

## Using AI to Draft, Not Fabricate

- **Structure:** "Suggest a homepage hero line for a [role] portfolio — 5 options, courageously honest."
- **Hooks:** "Write 3 one-line openings for each project description."
- **Rewrite:** "Make my About more specific and less generic — remove clichés like 'passionate,' 'highly motivated.'"
- **Review:** "Read my whole portfolio. List 3 improvements: clarity, scannability, and story consistency."

**Never** let AI invent achievements, numbers, or projects you can't defend.

## Make It Scannable in 30 Seconds

- **Above the fold:** name+role line, one hero sentence, 1 screenshot.
- **Projects as cards:** title, 1-line what, 3 tech tags, link.
- **No walls of text.** People skim; help them.
- **Live demo/video beats screenshots:** a 60-sec demo link is a hiring superpower.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel / a platform your field prefers.

## Honesty Rules That Protect You

- Every claim must survive "show me" — test, demo, or data to back it.
- If AI helped build it, **say so proudly** — "Built with AI assistance; logic/design decisions mine." This is now a *positive* signal, not a confession.
- No fake user counts, no inflated grades, no copied template passing as yours.
- One mismatch between portfolio and resume = instant credibility loss.

## The Feedback Loop

Before sending it anywhere:

1. **Get 2 readers** (a senior, a non-technical friend) to answer: *What does this person do? What stuck? What confused you?*
2. **Time yourself with a stranger:** let them click around 30 seconds, then explain back your story. If they can't, tighten.
3. Iterate 2 rounds max, then **launch it** — perfect is the enemy of visible.

## Key Takeaways

- Portfolio = one clear story, answerable in 30 seconds.
- 2–3 projects, STAR-framed, all on-message.
- AI drafts and reviews; you verify every claim.
- Scannable cards + live demo + honest AI-use line beat essay-length pages.
- Launch early, gather 30-second feedback, tighten in 2 rounds.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Story sentence:** Write your "I solve ___ with ___" line. Test it on two people: does it instantly describe you?
2. **Pick 2–3 projects** from school/labs/this course, and write STAR mini case studies (AI-assisted, then edited by you).
3. **Build or rebuild** your portfolio one-pager with scannable cards, hero line, and contact, and host it somewhere shareable.
4. **Honesty audit:** Re-read every claim and mark ✓ if you can back it with a demo, data, or repo. Remove or soften anything not backed.
5. **30-second test:** have someone browse your portfolio for 30 seconds and say your story back to you.
