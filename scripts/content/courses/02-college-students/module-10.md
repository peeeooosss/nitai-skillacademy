## AI-Assisted Interview Practice

Interview prep is where AI is genuinely elite. Build a session:

**Round 1 — Role-specific questions:**
> "Act as an interviewer for a [role] at a [company type]. Ask me 5 behavioural and 3 technical questions, one at a time. After each answer, score me 1–5 and ask one follow-up."

**Round 2 — Projects under fire:**
> "Here's my project [paste STAR case study]. Grille me like a tough interviewer: 5 hard questions about my choices, trade-offs, and failures."

**Round 3 — Live skills demo:**
> "Ask me about the tool I used for [project]. Probe: why this method, what went wrong, what would I change?"

Rules that make it work:
- **Answer out loud**, not in your head — speech changes everything.
- **Let AI be rough.** Get comfortable being challenged.
- Combine with one **real mock interview partner** — AI can't substitute humans entirely.
- After every session, write down 3 weak spots and fix them before the next round.

## Presenting Your Project Live

Campus interviews and portfolio reviews love live demos:

- 90-second demo: problem → what you built → 20-second live run → result → what you'd improve.
- **Practice failure mode:** plan what to say if the demo crashes ("Let me walk you through what it does while it restarts").
- Hit **one technical question with depth.** Know your stack's 3 trade-offs cold.
- End with **one honest lesson** — interviewers remember humility + learning.

## The Ethics Framework (Your Permanent Guide)

Four test questions before shipping anything AI-assisted:

1. **Honesty:** Did I disclose where AI helped? Are all numbers true?
2. **Ownership:** Can I defend every line/decision? (If you can't explain it, you shouldn't claim it.)
3. **Privacy:** Does it ever touch personal data that shouldn't be there?
4. **Harm:** Could this mislead, bias, or hurt someone? (Bias check: whose perspective is missing?)

If all four pass, it's ethical to ship. This framework outlasts any single tool or policy.

Run this checklist once; keep it updated each semester. It's your career ammunition.
