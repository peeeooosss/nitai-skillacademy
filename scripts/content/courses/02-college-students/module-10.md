### Module 2 · AI-Assisted Interview Practice
Rehearse with AI like a real drill, not a script:

1. **Build a question bank:** *"Give me 20 likely questions for a {role} internship interview, including 5 behaviourals and 3 technicals about my own project."*
2. **Simulate a live interviewer:** *"Interview me one question at a time. After each answer, give brief feedback and the next question."*
3. **Record your best answers** and practise delivering them under 60 seconds each.

Then do a **real human mock** (friend, mentor, professor). AI covers volume; a human covers the awkward parts. Volume + authenticity = confident.

### Module 3 · Presenting Your Project Live
A live demo beats a rehearsed speech. Present like this:

- **60-second pitch:** the problem → your build → the result → what you'd do next.
- **Walk the core flow live** — the one that always works.
- **Show one thing that broke**, and how you fixed it (it humanises and demonstrates judgment).
- **End with a question:** "Want to see how I'd scale it?" invites the conversation you want.

Practise the pitch 3 times out loud with a timer. Then present it to a friend before the real room. Live Q&A is where your honesty rules (Mission 9) pay off in full.

### Module 4 · The Ethics Framework (Your Permanent Guide)
Carry one framework that answers any AI-ethics question:

- **Transparency:** when AI helped, say how.
- **Ownership:** you remain accountable for what you produce with AI.
- **Fairness:** don't let AI amplify bias in data or decisions.
- **Verification:** every fact and number you present is traced to a source.
- **Judgment:** AI suggests; the human decides.

Memorise these five words — **transparency, ownership, fairness, verification, judgment** — and run any dilemma through them. That 10-second filter answers nearly every AI-ethics question professors and hiring managers throw at students.

### Module 5 · Your Placement-Ready Package
Assemble the four deliverable pieces:

1. **Portfolio link** (Mission 9) — ready and current.
2. **60-second project pitch** — rehearsed with AI and a human.
3. **Interview question bank + STAR answers** — written in your voice.
4. **AI-usage statement** — one neat paragraph for CVs, reports, and application forms.

Check each against your university's placement requirements and verify it's honest. Then do a final run: *open your portfolio, demo the project, answer 3 behavioural questions.* If all three feel smooth, you're placement-ready.

### Module 6 · Your Next 90 Days (After Graduation)
Graduation is not the end of the learning loop — the skills compound if you keep them fed:

- **Weeks 1-4:** keep shipping one small thing a month; update your portfolio with each.
- **Months 2-3:** join one community (course alumni, open source, a LinkedIn niche group) and make one visible contribution.
- **Set a "learning budget":** 2 hours a week on one AI skill beats 5 hours once a month.
- **Re-run your ethics filter on every new tool** before you adopt it.

The students who keep the loop alive after the course outpace everyone who stopped at the certificate. Your next 90 days are the launch of a lifelong skill stack — start them on purpose.
