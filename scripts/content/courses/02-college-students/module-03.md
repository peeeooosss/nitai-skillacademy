# Mission 3: AI for Research & Literature Review

## Learning Objectives
- Build a research question and search strategy with AI
- Use AI to plan a literature review without skipping real sources
- Summarise, compare, and synthesise papers like a reviewer
- Detect AI hallucinations and verify every source citation
- Manage references with AI-supported workflows

---

## Research: Where AI Helps (and Where It Must Not)

Research is where AI shines — and where it's most dangerous. AI is superb at **organising thinking and language**; LLMs are unreliable at **creating real citations**. The professional rule:

> **AI for thinking, scholars for sources.** Let AI help you find, read, and compare papers. Never let AI invent the paper.

## Step 1: Sharpen Your Research Question

Start fuzzy and use AI to sharpen:

- "I'm studying [broad topic]. Generate 8 focused research questions that are specific, arguable, and feasible for a 6-week project."
- "Which question is most answerable with accessible data? Rank the 8 by difficulty and data availability."
- "For my top question, what are 3 sub-questions?" — this becomes your literature map.

## Step 2: Build a Search Strategy

A good search strategy finds real, relevant papers.

- **Get keywords:** "Give me 10 search keywords and 5 Boolean phrases for [question]."
- **Find databases:** ask which databases fit your field (PubMed, Scopus, IEEE Xplore, JSTOR, arXiv, Westlaw...).
- **Find the frontier:** "Name 5 landmark papers and 5 recent (last 3 years) papers on [question]. Give author + year + full title."
- **VERIFY 100% of papers yourself** on Google Scholar / the library. Never cite a paper you have not opened.

## Step 3: Read and Annotate With AI

Reading 20 papers is heavy. Use AI to triage, never to decide for you:

- **Triage:** Paste the abstract + conclusion of each paper and ask: "Relevance to my question: high/medium/low? In 2 lines, what does it contribute?" Build your shortlist.
- **Compare:** "Here are 5 papers on [topic]. Create a table: authors | year | method | key finding | limitation | relevance to my question."
- **Synthesise:** "Synthesise the table into 3 research themes and 1 unresolved gap."

> **Good practice:** Ask for limitations and disagreements — a literature review that only agrees isn't a review.

## The Verification Rule (Non-Negotiable)

Hallucinated citations are the #1 AI research trap. A fake paper can look perfect. Protect yourself:

- **DOI check:** Ask AI for the DOI, then look it up. No DOI → treat with suspicion.
- **Open every source:** If you can't open the paper, you can't cite it.
- **Use retrieval tools** (like a research copilot with citations) that actually return real sources — and still spot-check.
- **Square brackets:** mark every AI-suggested claim as a "to-verify" placeholder until confirmed.

## Manage References Like a Pro

- Use a **reference manager** (Zotero/Mendeley) from day one — export BibTeX/CSL for your word processor.
- **AI can format, not fabricate:** "Format these 5 references in APA 7 from the details I give you" (paste real details). Then compare with a formatting guide.
- **Annotate as you read:** one sentence per paper: contribution + limitation. This literally writes your review later.

## Key Takeaways

- AI organises research thinking; real scholars + databases supply sources.
- Sharpen questions and searches with AI, then search yourself.
- Use AI for triage, comparison tables, and synthesis — always keep final judgment.
- Verify every citation (DOI + open the paper). Assume hallucination until proven.
- Reference managers + clean annotations = half the review written in advance.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Question sharpen:** Go from broad topic → 8 research questions → top 1 + 3 sub-questions.
2. **Search strategy:** Build 10 keywords + 3 Boolean phrases, run them in 2 databases, and keep the top 5 real papers.
3. **Comparison table:** Use AI to make a compare table for those 5 papers — then personally verify 2 of them by opening them and correcting any AI error.
4. **Set up Zotero/Mendeley:** Add those 5 papers with clean metadata and export one APA 7 reference correctly.
