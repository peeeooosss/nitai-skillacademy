### Module 1 · The Four-Question Rhythm
The most valuable outcome of this course is not one trick - it is a **system**: a repeatable monthly rhythm where AI informs decisions and official sources verify them. Build it once, run it every season.

Every important farm decision answers four questions. AI helps you prepare all four; official sources confirm the final two:

1. **What's the situation?** - weather, soil, crop health, market.
2. **What are my options?** - sowing, protection, selling routes, products.
3. **What's the cost and risk?** - money, labour, water, season.
4. **What's the official truth?** - KVK/agri office/market/scheme verification.

Prompt: "My farm: [crop, acres, soil, irrigation]. For this time of year: today's weather picture, my options, and the risks for each - so I can decide."

**Try it now:** Run the four-question rhythm on your next real farm decision.

### Module 2 · Your Farm-Year Calendar
Map the year in four chunks, and attach an AI check to each:

- **Pre-sowing:** soil health, seed choice, seasonal weather outlook, scheme applications.
- **Growing:** crop advisory on demand, pest alerts, irrigation and fertiliser decisions.
- **Harvest:** the right timing, grading, moisture management.
- **Selling:** price comparison (mandi/eNAM/direct), scheme claims, value-added trials in the surplus season.

Each month, spend one Sunday-morning hour: AI session + write the plan + verify one official thing.

**Try it now:** Draw four boxes on a page - sow, grow, harvest, sell - and note the one AI check you will run for each.

### Module 3 · Your Weekly AI Habits
Set habits that run themselves:

- **Sunday:** weather + crop check for the week. Ask: "What should I watch in my [crop] this week, given the forecast?"
- **On alert:** any unusual pest/leaf change → photo + AI + official confirmation.
- **Fortnightly:** market price check for your main crop.
- **Monthly:** one scheme or registration check, one value-addition idea test.

These small regular habits compound into a well-informed farm year - better sowing dates, fewer losses, and more schemes claimed.

**Try it now:** Write your weekly habit list and pin it near where you charge your phone.

**The compounding effect:** In one year, you will have personally guided 12 neighbours. Each of them may guide others. This is how a village raises itself.
