# Mission 2: Weather, Soil & Crop Advisory with AI

Weather and soil are the two biggest risks in farming. This mission shows you how to get dependable weather guidance, understand your soil, and turn AI advice into a practical crop plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Get a clear 7-day and seasonal weather picture for your own village area.
- Understand your soil type and what it means for sowing and fertiliser.
- Ask AI for a season-specific crop advisory for your district.
- Learn how to double-check AI advice with official sources like Kisan Suvidha and MSSRF advisory services.

## The Core Idea

Farming advice is only as good as the information behind it. The best habits combine three sources:

1. **Official advisories** — governments and institutions publish zone-specific crop and weather advisories.
2. **Farm apps** — free apps designed for Indian agriculture (Kisan Suvidha, Pusa Krishi, mKisan, IMD apps, district agri portals).
3. **General AI assistants** — which can interpret all the above for you and answer your plain-language questions instantly.

AI is a lens: it takes data (weather, crop conditions, your description) and turns it into simple, actionable advice for your farm.

### Weather That Speaks Your Language

Weather apps give raw forecasts; AI gives decisions. Instead of hunting through a dashboard, ask:

- "For [your taluk/district], this week's weather — will there be rain days or heat days? How should this affect my spraying schedule?"
- "It looks like thunderstorms in three days. Should I harvest the paddy before or wait?"
- "This monsoon seems weak this year. Which crops cope better with light rain?"

### Soil: Know Your Land

- Learn your soil type: **black (regur), red, alluvial, loam, sandy, saline?** — observe where it gets sticky in rain, how fast it dries, how water drains.
- Ask AI: "How do I test my soil simply as a small farmer? What do pH, N-P-K, and organic matter mean — in simple words?"
- Apply this to sowing: "What grows best in red soil with good drainage and no canal water?"
- Treat official soil health card guidance as the authority (your agri department issues them) — AI explains it.

### Building a Season Crop Plan With AI

A good season plan answers: **what crop, what land, what cost, what care, what risk.**

Prompt example: *"Act as an agricultural advisor for [district], [season]. I have [acres] of [soil type] land with [irrigation]. What should I sow this season? Compare 2–3 options: investment, labour, expected yield, profit, water need, and risks. Keep it practical for a small farmer."*

Then follow up: "What fertiliser plan for option 2?" and "What are the main pests for this crop and the first sign to watch?"

### Safety First — The "Check Twice" Rule

Never apply pesticides, fertilisers, or treat disease based on one AI answer alone. Always:

- Cross-check with **official sources**: your local KVK (Krishi Vigyan Kendra), district agriculture office, or a trusted agronomy app.
- Confirm product names, doses, and withdrawal periods before applying.
- For live disease detection, use a photo-based advisory tool (plantix-style / official apps) which was trained on crop images — then consult an expert for serious outbreaks.

## Real-World Examples

**Example 1 – The spraying decision.** Ravi asked AI: "I need to spray for pod borer in the next week. Rain is expected Wednesday. What's the best day, and what's the safest time of day?" Combining the forecast with official advice, he sprayed Tuesday morning — before the rain — and saved the dressing from washing away.

**Example 2 – The soil discovery.** Sunita's fields dried fast after rain. Asking AI "why does my soil dry so quickly and what does that mean", she learned her soil was sandy-loam with poor water retention. The advice — more organic matter, drip lines, and crops like groundnut — matched the KVK visit the same month.

**Example 3 – The season plan.** Dattatray asked AI to compare soybean and pulses for his district. The AI outlined investment, expected yields, and water needs, flagging soybean as high-upside but higher-risk in a dry year. He chose a mixed plan and discussed it with the agri officer before sowing a single seed.

## Key Takeaways

- Combine official advisories, farm apps, and AI — no single source is enough.
- Weather advice becomes decisions: when to sow, spray, harvest, or hold.
- Know your soil; AI explains it and turns it into a crop plan.
- A good season prompt asks about crop, land, cost, care, and risk together.
- Check twice before touching money, chemicals, or serious treatments: cross-check with KVK or the district agriculture office.

## Practice Challenge

1. Ask AI for your district's 7-day weather forecast summary in simple language.
2. Find out (observe or ask) your main soil type and confirm with AI what it means for your land.
3. Ask AI the full season-plan prompt for your next sowing — and get 2–3 compared options.
4. Now check: visit one official source (Kisan Suvidha app, your KVK, district agri office) and verify at least one recommendation.
5. Write your one-page season plan: crop choice, input cost, care calendar, and your top risk.
