# Mission 3: Market Prices & Government Schemes via AI

## Learning Objectives

- Get the current mandi price picture for your crop across nearby mandis
- Understand why prices differ enough to decide where to sell
- Use AI to find government schemes for your situation and how to apply
- Follow the golden safety rule: never trust money advice without official verification

### Module 1 · Know Today's Price Picture
Farmers historically decide what to sell, where, and to whom with thin information. AI compresses days of phone calls and mandi visits into a ten-minute planning session.

- Use official price information first: **eNAM (National Agriculture Market)**, state mandi portals, and the Agmarknet mandi price system.
- Ask AI to interpret: "What is the recent trend of [crop] prices in [my mandi] and [nearby mandi]? What causes the difference - season, supply, or quality grades?"
- Remember: AI does not have live mandi data by itself - treat its answers as analysis questions, and verify actual figures on official apps or portals.

The point is not that AI gives you prices. The point is that AI helps you understand the prices you look up.

**Try it now:** Look up today's price for your main crop on an official source, then ask AI to explain the trend and difference between your mandi and one nearby.

### Module 2 · Why Prices Differ
Prices differ for clear reasons. Understanding them helps you decide where and when to sell:

- **Quality & grades** - better graded produce earns more; grading matters.
- **Season & supply** - harvest glut lowers prices; off-season raises them.
- **Mandi vs eNAM** - eNAM auctions across districts for transparent discovery; local mandis are cash-fast but often lower.
- **Distance & transport** - a mandi far away may pay more but cost more to reach.

Ask AI: "Compare selling my [crop] at [local mandi] vs eNAM vs direct. What are the real costs - transport, commission, waiting, grading - and when does each make sense?"

Then **verify the mandi price** for today on the official app before negotiating.

**Try it now:** List your crop's selling options and ask AI to compare them with real costs for your distance.

### Module 3 · Find Your Government Schemes
Schemes change every year and differ by state, so AI can't give you perfect facts - but it is brilliant at *finding and explaining* candidates to verify.

Ask AI:
> "List government schemes a [small/marginal] farmer with [acres] of [crop/orchard] in [state] can apply for this year. For each: what it gives, who is eligible, and how to apply. Mark which are most practical for me."

Expect candidates like:

- **PM-KISAN** - direct income support instalments.
- **PMFBY / crop insurance** - premium support against crop loss.
- **KCC (Kisan Credit Card)** - loan support at subsidised rates.
- **State input subsidy schemes** - seeds, fertiliser, machinery, drip.
- **eNAM / mandi modernisation registrations.**

Then do the "double check": visit the official portal (PM-KISAN, PMFBY, your state agri website) or the agri office to confirm eligibility and dates before applying.

### Module 4 · The Golden Money Rules
Money advice deserves the strictest checks:

1. **Verify on official sources** before acting - AI analysis is a plan, not proof.
2. **Never pay anyone to "help you apply"** for a scheme. Government schemes are free to apply; anyone charging a fee is a scam.
3. **Never share OTPs or bank details** over the phone, even from a "government officer" caller.
4. **Record every application number** and keep copies of all papers.
5. **Ask your agri officer** - they are paid to help you with exactly this.

These five rules protect families every harvest season. Schemes are free to apply - anyone charging is a scam.

**Try it now:** Write these five rules in your phone notes. Keep them there permanently.

### Module 5 · The Mandi Decision Walkthrough
A complete money decision combines prices, options, and verification:

1. **Check the official price** for your crop today (eNAM/Agmarknet/state portal).
2. **Ask AI to compare** selling routes - local mandi vs eNAM vs direct - with real costs: transport, commission, waiting, grading.
3. **Verify findings** with the official app or a call to the agri officer.
4. **Decide with facts:** one small test lot where the comparison is close.
5. **Record the result** in your notes - your own price history becomes your best asset.

Even a partial application of this habit improves your bargaining position. You now know what good looks like.

### Module 6 · Schemes, Numbers, and Records
Getting money from schemes is a paperwork discipline:

- **Apply yourself or with your agri officer** - free of charge, never through a fee-charging agent.
- **Note every application number** - write it down immediately, in a notebook or phone note.
- **Keep copies of all papers** - applications, acknowledgement slips, passbook, Aadhaar where relevant.
- **Respect the dates** - schemes have windows; missing them costs a year.
- **Follow up politely** - after a reasonable wait, ask the officer for status.

**Key takeaway:** Money decisions are where farmers gain or lose most. AI finds and explains options; official sources and good records turn them into real money.

**Example Quiz**

## Key Takeaways

- Price decisions are better with a market analysis (mandi vs eNAM vs direct) - verify actual figures officially.
- AI is excellent at finding and explaining schemes; only official portals and officers confirm them.
