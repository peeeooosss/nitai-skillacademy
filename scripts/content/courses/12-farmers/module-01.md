# Mission 1: Welcome — How AI Helps Farmers

A warm and practical welcome to the Kisan AI course. By the end of this session you will know, in plain language, what AI is, how it already helps Indian farmers every day, and what it can concretely do for your own farm.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain AI in one simple sentence you could say to a fellow farmer.
- Name five ways AI already helps Indian agriculture today.
- Understand how to ask an AI assistant about your own farm.
- Know that you need no English and no computer to begin — just a phone.

## The Core Idea

**AI (Artificial Intelligence) is a helper on your phone that understands questions in plain language and answers instantly.** Think of it as a very knowledgeable field assistant who has studied weather data, thousands of crop advisories, market price reports, and government schemes — and can talk to you in your own language.

You talk to it through a **chat assistant**: an app where you type or speak "What crop should I sow in this soil in October?" and it gives you a considered answer in seconds.

### How AI Already Helps Indian Agriculture

1. **Weather & sowing advice** — apps and voice assistants warn about rain/heat and suggest the right sowing time.
2. **Crop & pest alerts** — AI scans crop photos for disease or pest damage and recommends action.
3. **Best market prices** — daily mandi price information, so you know which mandi pays best for your produce.
4. **Government schemes** — help finding and understanding schemes like PM-KISAN, crop insurance, and subsidies.
5. **Fertiliser & soil guidance** — plans matched to your soil and crop, reducing waste and cost.

### What This Means for You

The machines are not coming to replace farmers — they are coming to stand beside you like a helper who reads faster and remembers more. The skill you need is simple: **ask clearly, check carefully, and apply with your own judgement.** This course trains exactly that skill, on your phone, in your language.

### Two Ways to Use AI on Your Farm

- **Special farm apps** — like Kisan Suvidha, Pusa Krishi apps, and crop-specific advisory services (you'll learn to choose carefully in Mission 2).
- **General AI assistants** — like ChatGPT, Gemini, or a voice assistant, answering your own free-form questions about your farm.

### The Golden Rule

AI gives suggestions, not commands. **You know your land, your season, and your budget.** Use AI's information as advice to check against your own experience and official sources — especially for money matters like loans or insurance.

## Real-World Examples

**Example 1 – The sowing question.** Balu asked his phone assistant: "In October, after the rains, what should I sow in black cotton soil in my district? I have canal water." The AI gave three options with their water, labour, and profit outlook — a fast starting point he then discussed with the local agri officer.

**Example 2 – The price check.** Meena reads mandi prices on her phone every Friday. AI now compiles the week's trend for her crop in nearby mandis and flags which one was paying best — a routine her family used to spend an afternoon gathering.

**Example 3 – The scheme search.** Knowing there were schemes he'd not claimed, Dattatray asked: "Which government schemes can a small farmer with 2 hectares of orchard apply for this year?" The AI listed likely ones with eligibility and how to verify them at the agri office — saving him many trips.

## Key Takeaways

- AI is a fast, knowledgeable assistant that talks in your language on your phone.
- It helps with weather, crops, pests, prices, and schemes — everywhere around you.
- Special farm apps and general AI assistants are both useful; you will learn to pick per task.
- You bring the judgement; AI brings the information. Always cross-check money advice.
- No English or computer needed — a phone and this course are everything.
