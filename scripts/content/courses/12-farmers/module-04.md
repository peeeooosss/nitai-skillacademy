- Use WhatsApp and voice input to ask AI questions without typing

### Module 1 · Two Habits Turn Your Phone Into a Farm Assistant
You don't need to learn typing. Two everyday habits turn your phone into a farm assistant:

1. **Voice input** - tap the microphone in your AI app and speak in your own language; the app writes and answers your question.
2. **WhatsApp + AI services** - official agri services and well-run community groups bring advisories and real farmer answers to your existing chats.

Most farmers already live inside WhatsApp. The tools you use every day - WhatsApp groups and your phone's voice - become AI-powered farming tools.

**Try it now:** Open an AI app, tap the mic, and speak one farming question in your own language.

### Module 2 · Voice Is Your Fastest Skill
- Open ChatGPT, Gemini, or a voice-first assistant.
- Tap the mic and speak naturally: "My chilli leaves are turning yellow - what should I do?"
- The assistant answers in the same language, fast.

Voice removes the last barrier between you and the tool. Practice speaking longer questions - the tool handles full sentences better than single words.

**Why full sentences matter:** "my chilli leaves yellow, second week after transplanting, red soil, I water every morning" gives far better answers than "chilli yellow".

**Try it now:** Ask three farm questions with voice across the week - one weather, one crop, one market. This builds the habit.

### Module 3 · AI as Your Group Moderator
Village WhatsApp groups swing between two extremes: useless forwards and genuinely useful advisories. AI turns the chaos into structure:

- Ask for the day's reading of a message: "Summarise this news in 3 points: [paste text]. Is it likely true or forward-misinformation?"
- Ask before you forward anything money-related: "Is this scheme real or a scam? What are the official words I should search?"
- Turn group questions into advisory drafts: "Write a short message in my language warning the group about the fruit-fly alert from KVK."

You become the person who reads group messages with a critical assistant - quietly valuable.

**Try it now:** Take one forward from your group and ask AI to summarise it and judge its trustworthiness.

### Module 4 · The Reliable Official Partners
Pair AI with these free official channels:

- **Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551)** - call and get an answer from a real agriculture expert in your language.

- **KVK and agri officer mobile numbers** - your local experts are one message away.

**Rule of thumb:** AI is instant general advice. Official services are precise, verified advice. Use AI to ask *what to ask*, then confirm with the official answer for anything important.

### Module 5 · Protecting Your Village from Harmful Forwards
Wrong advice about pesticides, seeds, or "miracle schemes" costs real money and crops. As you become more skilled:

- Cross-check any alarming advice before passing it on.
- Attach the verified source when you share: "KVK says..."
- Flag obvious scams to group admins.
- Teach one other farmer what you learned this mission - that is how the village gets safer, farmer by farmer.

The **multiplier effect:** every farmer you teach improves the safety of the whole group. Your skill is not only for you.

**Try it now:** Write one verified advisory message for your group about this week's weather or crop, with its official source.
