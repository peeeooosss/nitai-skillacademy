### Module 1 · Plan the Shoot Before You Press Record

Most wasted time happens before recording: unclear goals, missing shots, and endless retakes. A short plan fixes that.

Planning the shoot with AI takes five minutes and saves an hour of editing and reshooting.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan a shoot before recording
- Define the goal and shots
- Reduce retakes and reshoots
- Use AI to plan

## Why Plan

- Unclear goals cause endless retakes
- Missing shots force reshoots
- A plan makes editing easier
### Why
- Planning saves time twice
- Fewer takes, cleaner edits
- Clear goals keep you on track

## The Plan

- Write the goal in one line
- List the shots you need
- Note the location, light, and audio
### The rule
- Plan before you record
- Know the goal in one line
- List every shot you need

## The Shoot-Plan Prompt

```text
I am filming a [length] [platform] video about [topic].
Give me a shoot plan: the goal in one line, a shot list,
and notes on location, light, and audio.
```
- Goal in one line
- A full shot list
- Location and audio notes

## Key Takeaways

- Most wasted time happens before recording
- Plan the shoot in five minutes to save an hour
- Write the goal in one line
- List every shot you need
- Note location, light, and audio

## Practice Challenge

1. Plan your next shoot in five minutes
2. Write the goal in one line
3. List every shot you need

**Example Quiz**

- B) In editing

**Answer:** A — Unclear goals and missing shots waste time before recording.

2. What does planning save?
- A) Time twice: shooting and editing
- B) Money only
- C) Nothing
- D) Only effort

**Answer:** A — A plan reduces takes and makes editing easier.

3. What should you write first?
- A) The goal in one line
- B) The caption
- C) The title
- D) The hashtags

**Answer:** A — A one-line goal keeps the shoot focused.

4. What should you note about the shoot?
- A) Location, light, and audio

**Answer:** A — Noting location, light, and audio prevents problems.

### Module 2 · Step 1: The Shot List and B-Roll

A shot list is every shot you need, in order. B-roll is the extra footage that covers cuts and adds interest.

With a list, you shoot once and move on. Without it, you edit around holes.

## Learning Objectives

- Build a shot list
- Plan b-roll to cover cuts
- Shoot enough coverage
- Avoid missing shots

## The Shot List

- Every shot you need, in order
- A-roll: you talking
- B-roll: supporting visuals
### Why
- A list prevents missing shots
- B-roll covers cuts and edits
- Shooting once saves time

## Planning B-Roll

- List visuals for each point
- Grab extra angles while you are there
- Keep clips short and usable
### The rule
- Shoot the list in order
- Always grab b-roll
- More coverage, easier edits

## The Shot-List Prompt

```text
For my [platform] video about [topic], give me a shot list
in order, plus b-roll ideas for each point that cover cuts
and add interest.
```
- An ordered shot list
- B-roll per point
- Coverage for edits

## Key Takeaways

- A shot list is every shot you need, in order
- A-roll is you talking; b-roll supports it
- B-roll covers cuts and adds interest
- Shoot the list in order and grab extra angles
- More coverage makes editing easier

## Practice Challenge

1. Build a shot list for your next video
2. Add three b-roll ideas per point
3. Shoot in list order

**Example Quiz**

1. What is a shot list?
- A) Every shot you need, in order
- B) A caption list
- C) A song list
- D) A hashtag list

**Answer:** A — A shot list keeps the shoot organised and complete.

2. What is b-roll?
- A) Supporting visuals
- B) You talking
- C) The caption
- D) The thumbnail

**Answer:** A — B-roll covers cuts and adds visual interest.

3. Why grab extra angles?
- A) More coverage makes editing easier
- B) To waste time
- C) To fill storage
- D) To confuse editors

**Answer:** A — Extra coverage gives you options when editing.

4. What does a list prevent?
- A) Missing shots
- B) Editing
- C) Publishing
- D) Captions

**Answer:** A — A shot list ensures you capture everything you need.

### Module 3 · Step 2: Auto-Captions and Subtitles

Captions are no longer optional: most people watch on mute. Auto-captioning tools turn your audio into text in seconds.

AI captions are fast but not perfect - always proofread before publishing.

## Learning Objectives

- Add captions to every video
- Use auto-caption tools
- Proofread AI captions
- Style captions for readability

## Why Captions

- Most viewers watch on mute
- Captions boost retention
- They make content accessible
### Why
- Silent viewing is the norm
- Accessibility widens your audience
- Retention rises with captions

## Using Auto-Captions

- Generate captions automatically
- Proofread names and jargon
- Style for size and contrast
### The rule
- Caption every video
- Always proofread
- Keep them readable

## The Caption Prompt

```text
Here is my video transcript [paste]. Fix the caption
punctuation and line breaks for [platform], and flag any
words likely misheard by auto-captions.
```
- Cleaned punctuation
- Readable line breaks
- Flagged mishearings

## Key Takeaways

- Most people watch on mute, so caption every video
- Captions boost retention and accessibility
- Auto-caption tools are fast but not perfect
- Always proofread names and jargon
- Style captions for size and contrast

### Module 4 · Step 3: AI-Assisted Editing (Cut, Silence, Filler)

AI editors can remove silences, filler words, and bad takes automatically. That turns an hour of editing into minutes.

Use AI for the mechanical cuts, then add the creative choices yourself.

## Learning Objectives

- Use AI to remove silence and filler
- Let AI handle mechanical cuts
- Keep creative choices yours
- Speed up editing

## What AI Cuts

- Silences and dead air
- Filler words like um and uh
- Bad takes and repeats
### Why
- Mechanical cuts are tedious
- AI does them instantly
- You focus on the story

## What You Keep

- Pacing and rhythm
- Which jokes and moments stay
- Music, graphics, and mood
### The rule
- AI cuts, you decide
- Review every AI cut
- Keep the creative choices

## The Editing Prompt

```text
My video transcript is [paste]. Suggest which silences,
filler words, and repeated takes to cut for a tight [length]
edit on [platform]. Keep the key points and best moments.
```
- Cuts to make
- Key points kept
- A tight runtime

## Key Takeaways

- AI editors remove silences, filler, and bad takes
- Let AI handle the mechanical cuts
- Keep pacing, jokes, and mood as your choices
- Review every AI cut before you commit
- Use AI to turn an hour of editing into minutes

## Practice Challenge

1. Run an AI edit on one raw clip
2. Review and adjust the cuts
3. Compare the time to manual editing

**Answer:** A — AI handles mechanical cuts like silences and filler words.

2. What stays your choice?
- A) Pacing, jokes, and mood

- C) Filler words
- D) Bad takes

**Answer:** A — Creative choices like pacing and mood stay with you.

3. What should you do with AI cuts?

**Answer:** A — Always review AI cuts before committing to them.

4. What does AI editing save?

### Module 5 · Step 4: Thumbnails and Covers That Convert

On many platforms the thumbnail or cover is what earns the click. It has to be clear, emotional, and readable at a glance.

AI helps generate ideas and concepts; the final choice must be honest and match the content.

## Learning Objectives

- Design thumbnails that earn clicks
- Use AI for thumbnail ideas
- Keep covers clear and honest
- Avoid misleading images

## What Works

- One clear subject or face
- High contrast and big text
- An emotion or curiosity gap
### Why
- Covers earn the click
- Clarity beats clutter
- Curiosity must be honest

## Using AI for Covers

- Generate concepts and layouts
- Test text options for clarity
- Check it reads on a small screen
### The rule
- One idea per cover
- Big, readable text
- Never mislead

## The Cover Prompt

```text
Give me 10 thumbnail concepts for a [platform] video about
[topic]: subject, emotion, text (3-4 words), and layout.
They must be clear at small size and honest to the content.
```
- Ten concepts
- Short text options
- Clear and honest

## Key Takeaways

- The thumbnail or cover earns the click
- Use one clear subject, high contrast, and big text
- Add an emotion or curiosity gap
- Generate concepts with AI and test readability
- Never mislead with the cover

### Module 6 · Step 5: The 30-Minute Edit

A repeatable edit process turns raw footage into a finished post in about half an hour: assemble, cut, caption, polish, export.

The goal is not a perfect edit - it is a clean, on-brand post, shipped on schedule.

## Learning Objectives

- Follow a repeatable edit process
- Edit in about 30 minutes
- Use AI for the slow parts
- Ship on schedule

## The Five Steps

- **Assemble:** lay clips in order
- **Cut:** remove silence and filler with AI
- **Caption:** add and proofread captions
- **Polish:** music, graphics, and pacing
- **Export:** correct format and settings
### Why
- A process removes guesswork
- AI handles the slow parts
- Shipping beats perfecting

## Keeping It Fast

- Work from the shot list
- Let AI do the mechanical cuts
- Timebox the polish
### The rule
- Same steps every time
- Timebox each stage
- Ship, then improve next time

## The Edit Prompt

```text
Walk me through a 30-minute edit for a [platform] video:
assemble, cut, caption, polish, export. Tell me where AI
helps most and how to timebox each step.
```
- Five edit steps
- Where AI helps most
- Timeboxes per step

## Key Takeaways

- Use a repeatable five-step edit process
- Assemble, cut, caption, polish, export
- Let AI handle the mechanical cuts
- Timebox each stage to stay fast
- Ship on schedule, then improve next time

## Practice Challenge

1. Edit one video using all five steps
2. Time each stage
3. Note where AI saved the most time

**Example Quiz**

**Answer:** A — Assemble, cut, caption, polish, and export form the process.

2. How long should a simple edit take?
- A) About 30 minutes

- D) Two minutes

**Answer:** A — A repeatable process keeps a simple edit around 30 minutes.

3. What should you timebox?
- A) Each stage

**Answer:** A — Timeboxing each stage keeps the edit fast.

4. What beats perfecting?
- A) Shipping on schedule
- B) Endless tweaks
- C) Delaying
- D) Deleting

**Answer:** A — Shipping a clean post beats perfecting forever.
