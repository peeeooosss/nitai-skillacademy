# Mission 7: AI Ethics — Privacy, Honesty, Real vs Fake

## Learning Objectives
- Protect your personal information when using AI
- Understand fake content: deepfakes, fake images, and fake news
- Learn the difference between "AI made it" and "it really happened"
- Practise honest and kind use of AI
- Build a personal AI Safety Code

---

## The AI Superpower Comes With Rules

AI is powerful — and with great power comes great responsibility. This mission is about using your AI superpower **safely, honestly, and kindly**. Let's learn the three protection shields.

## Shield 1: Protect Your Privacy

AI tools store your chats. Be careful what you type:

**Never share with AI:**
- Your full name + school name together
- Your home address, phone number, or birthday
- Photos of your face, or faces of family and friends
- Passwords, OTPs, or bank/app details (even if AI asks!)
- School login details

**Smart habits:**
- Use fake names in examples: "a student in Class 6" instead of your real name.
- Don't paste private letters or documents into AI.
- If an app asks for your photo, camera, or contacts — stop and ask a grown-up.
- Use apps that your school or parents approve.

> **Golden privacy rule:** *If you wouldn't write it on the class board, don't type it to AI.*

## Shield 2: Honesty — Always Tell the Truth About AI

Honesty means being clear when AI helped you:

- Write your schoolwork yourself, and be proud of using AI as a coach.
- If a project allows AI help, write a small line: "I used AI to brainstorm ideas; I wrote and designed the rest myself."
- Never print AI answers as your own on graded work without your teacher's permission.
- Never pretend an AI-made image or video is your own work.

**The honest user is never caught; the dishonest user always is.** And honesty keeps your learning real — which is the whole point.

## Shield 3: Real vs Fake — Become a Fact Detective

AI can create pictures, videos, and voices that look and sound completely real. These are called **deepfakes**. And AI can also write fake news that spreads fast.

**Famous fake traps:**
- 😱 "This famous person said THIS!" — a video with a real face and a fake voice.
- 📸 An amazing event photo that never happened.
- 📰 Shocking news headlines with no source.

**The Detective's Toolkit — F.A.C.T.**
- **F — Find the source.** Who made this? Is it a known, trusted site or an unknown account?
- **A — Ask more questions.** Does it match other trustworthy reports?
- **C — Check for clues.** Strange hands, blurry edges, wobbly lips, weird reflections? AI images often mess these up.
- **T — Tell a grown-up.** If something claims something shocking or asks you to share it urgently — STOP and ask an adult first.

**Bonus habit:** Use *reverse image search* (with a grown-up) to see where a picture really came from.

## The Kindness Rule

Words matter, even computer words:

- Don't ask AI to write mean, hateful, or scary things about anyone — not even "as a joke."
- Don't create fake images to embarrass or bully classmates. **This is serious and can hurt deeply** — never do it.
- If a friend is spreading fake content or cyberbullying, be brave and tell a trusted adult.

## Your Personal AI Safety Code

Make your own 5-line Safety Code. Start with these, then add your own:

1. I will never share my full name, school, address, or photos with AI or strangers online.
2. I will always check with a grown-up before using a new app or sharing something online.
3. I will do my own schoolwork and honestly say when AI helped me.
4. I will check F.A.C.T. before believing or sharing anything surprising.
5. I will keep my AI use kind, respectful, and safe for everyone.

## Key Takeaways

- Privacy: never give AI your real personal information.
- Honesty: be proud and truthful about how you used AI.
- Deepfakes exist: real-looking videos/photos can be fake — be a detective.
- Use F.A.C.T. to check anything surprising before believing or sharing.
- Kindness rule: never use AI to hurt, embarrass, or bully anyone.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Write Your Safety Code:** Complete your 5-line Personal AI Safety Code and pin it near your study desk.
2. **Spot the Fake (with a grown-up!):** Find 1 example of a famous deepfake or fake image discussion online together and talk about what clues gave it away.
3. **Privacy Audit:** Check the last 3 things you typed to AI (or think of 3 future prompts). Rewrite them so they contain NO personal information.
4. **Teach the Family:** Explain the F.A.C.T. detective toolkit to your parents or siblings in your own words.
