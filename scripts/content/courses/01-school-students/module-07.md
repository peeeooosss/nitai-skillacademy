### Module 2 · Shield 1: Protect Your Privacy

AI tools store your chats. That means whatever you type can live longer than the moment of asking - so you must be careful with the words and details you share. The golden privacy rule: *if you wouldn't write it on the class board, don't type it to AI.*

Protecting privacy is like cleaning your digital room - quick to do and keeps you safe. Tonight's privacy audit: look at the last few things you typed to AI and rewrite them with NO personal information.

## Learning Objectives

- List what you should never share with AI
- Use fake names in examples
- Follow the golden privacy rule
- Run a privacy audit on your recent prompts

## Never Share With AI

- Your full name + school name together
- Your home address, phone number, or birthday
- Photos of your face, or faces of family and friends
- Passwords, OTPs, or bank/app details (even if AI asks!)
- School login details

## Smart Privacy Habits

- Use fake names in examples: 'a student in Class 6' instead of your real name
- Don't paste private letters or documents into AI
- If an app asks for your photo, camera, or contacts - stop and ask a grown-up
- Use apps that your school or parents approve
```text
Golden privacy rule:
If you wouldn't write it on the class board,
don't type it to AI.
```

## Key Takeaways

- Never give AI your full name + school, address, phone, or photos
- Passwords and bank details stay out - even if AI asks
- Use fake names in examples - 'a student in Class 6'
- Golden rule: if you wouldn't write it on the board, don't type it

### Module 4 · Shield 3: Real vs Fake — Become a Fact Detective

AI can create pictures, videos, and voices that look and sound completely real. These are called **deepfakes**. And AI can also write fake news that spreads fast. To stay safe, you need the detective's toolkit - F.A.C.T.

Famous fake traps: a video with a real face and a fake voice; an amazing event photo that never happened; shocking news headlines with no source. Your job is to spot them and never spread them.

## Learning Objectives

- Define deepfake in your own words
- List three famous fake traps
- Use the F.A.C.T. toolkit to check surprising content
- Apply reverse image search with a grown-up

## What Is a Deepfake?

- Media changed by AI to show something fake
- Real face + fake voice saying what it never said
- An amazing event photo that never happened
- Shocking news headlines with no source

## The Detective's Toolkit — F.A.C.T.

- **F - Find the source.** Who made this? Is it a known, trusted site or an unknown account?
- **A - Ask more questions.** Does it match other trustworthy reports?
- **C - Check for clues.** Strange hands, blurry edges, wobbly lips, weird reflections - AI images often mess these up
- **T - Tell a grown-up.** If something claims something shocking or asks you to share urgently - STOP and ask an adult first
```text
Whisper the four letters:
F-A-C-T. Then act on it.
```

## Bonus: Reverse Image Search

### The bonus habit
- Search with a grown-up to see where a picture really came from
- Upload or screenshot the image in the search tool
- The results show who used the image before
- Comparing origins reveals fakes and edits

## Key Takeaways

- Deepfakes are real-looking media made to show something fake
- Fake traps: fake voices, fake photos, shocking no-source headlines
- F.A.C.T.: Find source, Ask questions, Check clues, Tell a grown-up
- AI images often show weird hands, blurry edges, and reflections
- Reverse image search (with a grown-up) traces where a picture came from

### Module 5 · The Kindness Rule

Words matter - even computer words. Don't ask AI to write mean, hateful, or scary things about anyone, not even 'as a joke.' And never create fake images to embarrass or bully classmates - that is serious and can hurt deeply.

Why it matters: the person 'behind the screen' is a real person with real feelings. A fake image or a mean AI text can humiliate someone for years. Kindness online is not soft - it's strength, and it protects everyone, including you.

## Learning Objectives

- State the kindness rule for AI use
- Explain why online people are real people
- Apply the kindness check before sharing
- Tell a trusted adult about online harm

## Never Mean, Never Harmful

- Don't ask AI to write mean, hateful, or scary things about anyone
- Not even 'as a joke'
- Never create fake images to embarrass or bully classmates
- This is serious and can hurt deeply - never do it

## The Person Behind the Screen

- Every 'user' or 'avatar' is a real person with real feelings
### The long-term cost
- A fake image or a mean AI text can humiliate someone for years
- Kindness online is not soft - it is strength
- It protects everyone, including you

## The Kindness Check

### Before you share anything, ask
- 'Would I be okay if this was written or shown about me?'
- If the answer is no, don't send it
```text
If a friend spreads fake content or
cyberbullying - be brave and tell
a trusted adult.
```

## Key Takeaways

- Never ask AI for mean, hateful, or scary content - even as a joke
- Never make fake images to embarrass or bully anyone
- The person behind the screen has real feelings
- Kindness check: 'Would I be okay if this was about me?'
- Be a brave friend - tell a trusted adult about cyberbullying

### Module 6 · Your Personal AI Safety Code

Make your own 5-line Safety Code. Start with these lines, then add your own. A code you can see is a code you remember - and codes work best when you review them every week.

Pin it somewhere visible: your study desk, your notebook cover, or the fridge. Your future self will thank you. These five lines are the difference between using AI as a superpower and being hurt by it.

## Learning Objectives

- Write a 5-line personal AI Safety Code
- Display the code somewhere visible
- Review the code weekly and keep your promises
- Explain how the code protects your future

## The Five Starting Lines

- **1.** I will never share my full name, school, address, or photos with AI or strangers online
- **2.** I will always check with a grown-up before using a new app or sharing something online
- **3.** I will do my own schoolwork and honestly say when AI helped me
- **4.** I will check F.A.C.T. before believing or sharing anything surprising
- **5.** I will keep my AI use kind, respectful, and safe for everyone

## Make It Visible and Review It

### Where to pin it
- Your study desk, your notebook cover, or the fridge
### The weekly review
- Every week, read your five lines
- Check you've kept each promise
```text
Safety code formula:
5 clear lines + a visible spot + weekly review
= habits that last a lifetime
```

## Your Future Self Will Thank You

- These five lines keep AI a superpower you control
### The alternative
- Without a code, careless use can hurt you
- With a code, AI stays your helpful assistant
- Reviewing keeps the promises fresh - the code becomes you

## Key Takeaways

- Write a 5-line personal AI Safety Code
- Include privacy, grown-up checks, honesty, F.A.C.T., and kindness
- Pin it somewhere visible - a seen code is a remembered code
- Review your code every week and keep each promise
- The code decides whether AI is a superpower or a danger
