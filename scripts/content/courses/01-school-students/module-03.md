# Mission 3: AI for Homework, Study Help & Projects

## Learning Objectives
- Use AI as a study buddy without breaking the honesty rules
- Learn the copy-and-explain method for homework help
- Create your own practice tests with AI
- Make study notes, summaries, and flashcards with AI
- Build a "study-with-AI" checklist you can use every day

---

## The Golden Rule of Study AI

Your teachers gave you homework so you can **learn**. AI should help you learn faster — never do your homework for you.

> **The copy-and-explain method:** If you give AI a question, always ask it to *explain how to solve it*, do the solving yourself, and then ask AI to check your work.

Copy-pasting answers makes you **weaker**, not stronger. On exam day, the AI won't be there. So use AI the way a great coach would: to practise, not to cheat.

## Way 1: The Copy-and-Explain Method

When you're stuck on homework:

1. **Type your problem clearly.** e.g., "Solve this and explain each step: 3x + 5 = 20."
2. **Ask AI NOT to give the final answer first.** Try: *"Don't tell me the answer. Give me the first step only, then wait."*
3. **Solve the next step yourself.**
4. **Ask AI to check:** "I solved it and got x = 5. Is that correct? Show me where I might have gone wrong."

This way YOU do the thinking, and AI is your checking partner. This is the **single best study habit** in this entire course.

## Way 2: Create Your Own Practice Tests

Tests feel scary when they surprise you. Practise with your own questions:

- **Prompt idea:** "Create 8 practice questions on [topic] for Class [your class]. Three multiple choice, three short answer, two word problems. Difficulty: medium. Put them in a table. Do not give the answers yet."
- **Then ask for the answer key:** "Now show the answer key separately and explain the trick behind each answer."

Revising by *answering questions* (not just reading) is scientifically proven to make you remember more. It's called **active recall** — and AI makes it super easy.

## Way 3: Summaries, Notes and Flashcards

Reading a huge chapter? Let AI turn it into study-friendly material:

- **Summary:** "Summarise this chapter in 5 bullet points, each under 15 words."
- **Plain English:** "Explain this paragraph like I'm 10 years old."
- **Flashcards:** "Turn these notes into 10 flashcards. Front: question. Back: answer."
- **Important words (Glossary):** "Make a glossary of the 8 hardest words in this chapter with one-line meanings."

⚠️ **Top Tip:** Paste your *own class notes* into the prompt, not random internet text. AI works best with YOUR material.

## Daily Study-with-AI Checklist

Before you start homework, run this checklist:

- [ ] I read the question myself and tried to understand it first.
- [ ] I wrote my prompt clearly (REC!).
- [ ] I asked AI to *explain*, not just answer.
- [ ] I did the real thinking and solving myself.
- [ ] I asked AI to check my work afterwards.
- [ ] I understood every line before moving on.

## What NOT to Do (Signals of Cheating)

- ❌ Copying AI text into homework, essays, or projects without understanding.
- ❌ Getting an answer and never checking if it's right.
- ❌ Using AI to write your whole assignment at the last minute.
- ❌ Sharing personal details (name, school, address, photos) with AI tools.
- ❌ Trusting every AI answer — AI is smart but can still be wrong. **You are the final checker.**

## Key Takeaways

- AI = personal study coach, not homework-answering machine.
- Use the **copy-and-explain method** to stay honest and learn more.
- Build your own **practice tests** to revise with active recall.
- Turn chapters into **summaries, flashcards, and glossaries**.
- After every AI session: check, understand, and do the thinking yourself.
