## Welcome to Your Grand Finale

You've climbed seven missions. Now comes the mission where you put it all together — your **AI Showcase Project**. This is the project you'll show your class, your family, or your teacher, and it proves everything you've learned.

> **The theme:** *One idea, made better with AI, finished by YOU.*

## Step 1: Choose Your Idea (Pick ONE)

Any of these work great:

- 🎨 **"Best Class Ever" Poster or Storybook** — a creative art project for your class.
- 🔢 **"My Favourite Subject" Report** — a school subject you explored deeply with AI and made a mini-guide for.
- 🌍 **"Save Our Earth / Water / Trees" Campaign** — an awareness poster + 1-page plan.
- 📖 **"A Day in the Future with AI"** — a short story with AI-generated pictures.
- ⚗️ **"My Experiment Diary"** — a safe home experiment explained step-by-step, with photos and an AI-written summary.

## Step 2: Plan Before You Build (Remember Mission 3!)

Use AI to plan — then do the work yourself:

1. **Outline prompt:** "Help me plan a [your project type] about [your idea] for a Class [class] student. Give me: goal, 3 parts, and what I will make myself vs what AI will help with."
2. **Storyboard it:** Decide each piece: title, text, images, and the message you want to send.
3. **Time plan:** Spread the work over 2–3 sessions. Big project + one night = stress. Small steps = success.

## Step 3: Build It With Your Ninja Skills

Now use every trick you learned:

- **Prompts (Mission 2):** Use Role → Explain → Check. Write clear, detailed prompts.
- **Study help (Mission 3):** Ask AI to explain ideas you don't understand before you write about them.
- **Creativity (Mission 4):** Generate images and drafts — then edit them until they're yours.
- **Presentation (Mission 6):** Write a short script to present your project.
- **Safety (Mission 7):** No personal info, no faces without permission, grown-up approves the app, honest on every slide.

## Step 4: The Honest AI Line on Every Project

Add one simple line at the end of your project:

> *"I used AI to help brainstorm ideas and drafts. I designed, checked, and built this project myself."*

This line makes you look **smart and trustworthy** — exactly the kind of student teachers love.

## Step 5: Present It Like a Pro

- Show the **before and after**: "Here's the AI's first draft, and here's what I improved."
- Explain **one thing you decided** and why.
- Tell them **one thing you learned** this course.
- Finish with your **signature** — your own proud touch.

## Practice Challenge — Your Final Project

1. **Choose** your project idea and get a parent/teacher approval.
2. **Plan** it with AI (outline + 3 parts) and write the plan in YOUR words.
3. **Build** it over 2–3 sessions, using at least 3 skills from this course.
4. **Add** your honest AI line and your signature.
5. **Present** it to your class, family, or teacher with a 2-minute talk.
