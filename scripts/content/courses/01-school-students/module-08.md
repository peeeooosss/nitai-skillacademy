### Module 3 · Step 2: Plan Before You Build (Remember Mission 3!)
Use AI to plan — then do the work yourself:

1. **Outline prompt:** *"Help me plan a [your project type] about [your idea] for a Class [class] student. Give me: goal, 3 parts, and what I will make myself vs what AI will help with."*
2. **Storyboard it:** Decide each piece: title, text, images, and the message you want to send.
3. **Time plan:** Spread the work over 2–3 sessions. Big project + one night = stress. Small steps = success.

**Sample time plan:**
- **Session 1:** Choose idea + AI outline + write the plan in your own words.
- **Session 2:** Build the content and images; edit everything.
- **Session 3:** Assemble, add your honest AI line + signature, practise your talk.

**Write the plan in YOUR words.** A plan you translate into your own language is a plan you actually understand — and that's the blueprint you'll follow with confidence.

### Module 4 · Step 3: Build It With Your Ninja Skills
Now use every trick you learned in this course:

- **Prompts (Mission 2):** Use Role → Explain → Check. Write clear, detailed prompts.
- **Study help (Mission 3):** Ask AI to explain ideas you don't understand before you write about them.
- **Creativity (Mission 4):** Generate images and drafts — then **edit them until they're yours**. Change at least 3 things and add one thing AI can't.
- **Presentation (Mission 6):** Write a short script to present your project.
- **Safety (Mission 7):** No personal info, no faces without permission, grown-up approves the app, honest on every slide.

**The 3-changes rule:** every AI draft you use should be edited at least three times — different colours, better words, new details. After three edits, the work has your fingerprint on it.

**And the golden rule from Mission 1 still stands:** AI helps you do the work better — AI never does YOUR work for YOU. Keep yourself as the boss from start to finish.

### Module 5 · Step 4: The Honest AI Line on Every Project
Add one simple line at the end of your project:

> *"I used AI to help brainstorm ideas and drafts. I designed, checked, and built this project myself."*

This line makes you look **smart and trustworthy** — exactly the kind of student teachers love.

**How to write yours:** be specific and proud. For example:
- *"AI helped me brainstorm 5 poster ideas; I chose the layout and drew the tree myself."*
- *"I used AI to draft the story; I rewrote the ending and added real photos."*

Specific honesty is even more impressive than the generic line — it shows you clearly understand your own work. And remember from Mission 7: honesty is your shield, and it protects your entire future reputation.
