### Module 3 · Step 2: Plan Before You Build (Remember Mission 3!)

Use AI to plan - then do the work yourself. A clear plan turns a big scary project into small easy steps. Your plan is the blueprint you will follow with confidence.

Write the plan in YOUR words. A plan you translate into your own language is a plan you actually understand - and understanding your plan is what makes it work.

## Learning Objectives

- Use an outline prompt to plan your project
- Storyboard each piece of the project
- Spread the work over 2-3 sessions
- Write the final plan in your own words

## The Outline Prompt

- 'Help me plan a [your project type] about [your idea] for a Class [class] student. Give me: goal, 3 parts, and what I will make myself vs what AI will help with'
- The outline gives you goal + structure + split of work

## Storyboard and Time Plan

- **Storyboard it:** decide each piece - title, text, images, and the message you want to send
- **Time plan:** spread the work over 2-3 sessions
### Sample sessions
- **Session 1:** choose idea + AI outline + write the plan in your own words
- **Session 2:** build the content and images; edit everything
- **Session 3:** assemble, add your honest AI line + signature, practise your talk

## Write the Plan in YOUR Words

- Translate the AI outline into your own language
### Why it matters
- A plan you understand is a plan you follow
- Your blueprint gives you confidence
```text
Plan equation:
Goal + 3 parts + your wording + 2-3 sessions = calm success
```

## Key Takeaways

- Use AI to plan: goal, 3 parts, and who does what

- Spread work over 2-3 sessions - big + one night = stress
- Write the final plan in your own words
- Small steps, followed calmly, beat last-minute scoring
