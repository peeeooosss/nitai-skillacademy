## What Is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is when a computer or a machine does things that normally need a **human brain** — like understanding your voice, answering questions, recognizing your face, or playing a game against you.

"Artificial" means "made by humans." "Intelligence" means "the ability to think and learn." So **AI is a machine that is taught to think and learn** — a bit like a very fast helper with a huge memory.

AI is not magic. It is **training and practice**. Just like you practise maths sums or swimming, AI learns from lots of examples. The more examples it sees, the smarter it gets at that one job.

## Where Is AI Around You?

You have probably used AI many times today without even knowing it. Can you spot which of these use AI?

- **Voice assistants** like Alexa, Siri, or Google — they listen, understand your words, and answer.
- **Games** — the computer opponent in your favourite game "thinks" and fights back.
- **Recommendations** — YouTube, Instagram, and music apps suggest videos you will like. That's AI learning from what you watch.
- **Spelling and grammar checkers** — they catch mistakes and suggest fixes.
- **Faces in photos** — when your phone draws a box around a face, that's AI.
- **Google Maps** — it picks the fastest route using AI.

These are all examples of **narrow AI** — AI that is very good at **one** job. AI that is good at many jobs, like human beings, does not exist yet. Today's AI is a specialist, not a superhero all-rounder.

## What Can AI Do for You as a Student?

AI can be an amazing **study buddy** if you use it smartly:

- Explain a topic in simple words when your teacher or textbook is confusing.
- Make practice questions to help you prepare for exams.
- Help you brainstorm ideas for stories, projects, and science fairs.
- Correct your English and improve your writing.
- Create pictures, slides, and videos for your school projects.

But remember this golden rule for the whole course:
> **AI helps you do the work better — AI should never do YOUR work for YOU.**

You are the boss. AI is your helper.

## The Three-Stop Rule for Using AI

Before you ask AI anything, stop and check:

1. **Stop — Plan first.** Think about what you really need. Write your question clearly.
2. **Stop — Read twice.** Never copy answers without understanding them. Ask AI to *explain*, not just give the answer.
3. **Stop — Check.** Does the answer make sense? Is it correct? Ask your teacher or parents if unsure.

You will practise this rule again and again in this course.

## Key Takeaways

- AI = machines that can think, learn, and do brain-jobs.
- Today's AI is "narrow" — super good at one specific job.
- AI is already all around you: games, phones, maps, and learning apps.
- AI is best used as a **helper**, never a replacement for your own thinking.
- Follow the Three-Stop Rule: Plan → Read twice → Check.
