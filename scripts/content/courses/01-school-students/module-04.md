# Mission 4: AI Art & Creative Projects

## Learning Objectives
- Generate images, stories, and poems with AI tools
- Write creative prompts that give you beautiful and unique results
- Edit and improve AI art to make it truly yours
- Understand that AI uses other artists' work and how to use it responsibly
- Create a finished creative project you can proudly share

---

## AI Can Create — But You Are the Artist

AI tools can create pictures, stories, poems, music, and even videos from just a prompt. This sounds like magic, but remember the golden rule:

> **AI is the paintbrush. YOU are the artist.**

The tool follows your instructions. Your imagination, your choices, and your edits make the project special. Two students with the same AI tool will make completely different projects — that's the power of being the artist.

## Writing a Great Creative Prompt

Creative prompts love **extra detail**. Use this recipe for pictures:

1. **What is it?** "A friendly robot watering flowers..."
2. **Where and when?** "...in a sunny school garden, in the morning..."
3. **How does it look?** "cartoon style, bright colours, soft shading..."
4. **Mood?** "peaceful and happy..."

**Example:**
> "A friendly robot watering flowers in a colourful school garden, cartoon style, bright colours, soft shading, sunny morning, peaceful mood — square image."

And for **stories**, try:
> "Write a 3-paragraph story for a 12-year-old about a girl who finds a library that only opens at midnight. Include a surprise ending. Use fun and simple language."

## Editing — Where Real Creativity Happens

The first result is rarely perfect. The best artists **iterate** (improve step by step):

- "Make the robot hold a book instead of a watering can."
- "Change the colours to purple and blue."
- "Make the story 2 paragraphs longer and add a second character."
- "Give the ending a happy twist."

You can also **remix** ideas: take a poem AI wrote, change the words you don't like, add your own, and make it yours. AI is a first draft machine — the final draft is always human.

## Creative Project Ideas (Pick One)

1. **Storybook Page:** A 1-page story with an AI-generated illustration for your younger class.
2. **Poster:** An awareness poster (e.g., Save Water, Anti-Litter, Book Week) with an AI image + your own message.
3. **Poem & Sky:** A short poem about your town with an AI sky-scene background.
4. **Animation Flip Book:** AI images of a bouncing ball at 8 stages — then flip through them fast!

## Responsible Art Rules (Very Important)

- **Be honest.** If a school project must be your own original work, ask your teacher. Never pass off AI art as 100% yours without telling.
- **No real people's faces** without permission — especially classmates, teachers, or family. Don't make embarrassing or scary images of anyone.
- **Don't copy a real artist's style with someone else's name** to pretend you made it.
- **AI learns from real artists' work.** That means you should support human artists too — enjoy their work, credit them, and never steal their final art.
- **No violence, hurtful, or rude content.** Keep your art kind and safe for everyone.
- **Check with a grown-up** before using any new creative site or app and before sharing your work online.

## Key Takeaways

- AI is the brush; you are the artist — your ideas + edits make it yours.
- Creative prompts need detail: what, where, look, and mood.
- Improve in steps ("iterating") until the result is something you love.
- Share AI-assisted work honestly and never with anyone's face without permission.
- Support and respect human artists; never present stolen work as yours.

## Practice Challenge

1. **Create:** Choose one creative project from the list above (or invent your own). Use AI to make a first draft image or story.
2. **Edit 3 Times:** Ask AI to change at least 3 things (colours, details, wording, characters). Save the final version.
3. **Be the Artist:** Add one thing AI can't add — your signature, a hand-written message, a photo element, or your own idea.
4. **Show & Tell:** Share your finished project with one smooth sentence about how you made it and what YOU decided. (No sharing online without a grown-up!)

Mission 4 done — you made real art, artist! 🎨
